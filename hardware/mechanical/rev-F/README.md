# Rev F engineering fit print

The power key faces outward on the top edge, 14 mm from the leftmost end with the display facing you. Press the engraved round cap inward. Its center is X −36.00, Y +34.32, Z 12.50 mm. The cap sits 0.18 mm inside the top-edge outline, protecting it during pocket carry. The same housing accepts the rigid slide-lock rod handle. With the handle detached, the rounded 3.20 mm rear shoe brings the stowed bounding thickness to 33.20 mm.

## Print and assemble

- Print `housing-top-power.stl`, `power-cap.stl`, and `key-board-retainer.stl` in the supplied orientations. The housing includes a rear dovetail receiver and an internal nut pocket. Support the back above the raised shoe and inspect the dovetail overhang in the slicer. Clear the receiver of supports and verify the central roof after printing. The cap prints flange down, with its power mark facing upward. A contrasting filament helps identify the cap by sight.
- Print the included bezel, rotor, folding stem, reel knob and collar. Fit the M3 receiver nut and its printed keeper before inserting the main electronics. For the removable rod handle, print the separate handle and screw keeper and install the M3 × 10 socket screw. Follow the [slide lock assembly and test procedure](ROD-GRIP.md).
- Order the 11 × 10 × 1.0 mm FR-4 carrier in the electronics files. Fit the gold-contact Omron B3F-1002-G, solder and trim its four leads, then attach the two insulated conductors. The 6.50 mm pin pitch runs along the 11 mm edge. Copper sits on isolated lands. Check the chosen contact pair with a meter. The fabrication holes are Ø1.00 mm finished; the nominal CAD envelope shows Ø1.10 mm holes. [Electrical assembly and harness](../../electronics/rev-f-power-key.md)
- Before installing the main controller, lower the power cap from the open display side, approximately 2.5 mm inboard of its final position. Push the cap toward the top edge into the counterbore. The retaining flange faces inward. Hold the cap out against its outer stop. Slide the daughterboard downward in the open tracks, actuator toward the cap. Route the two wires left of the main screw boss. The leads and insulation at the solder joints must fit in the 0.8 mm rear allowance. Strain-relieve the pair and keep it clear of the battery, antenna, fasteners and lid.
- Place the printed retainer over the board and install one M2 × 6 mm pan-head thread-forming screw for plastic into the Ø1.6 mm pilot. The model reserves a Ø4.0 × 1.6 mm screw head. Inspect the pilot and printed boss before tightening. Then fit the remaining Rev E hardware and wire the harness across the original PWR contacts. The original onboard key stays with its lower-edge service group.

## Closure and physical acceptance

The nominal cap has 0.25 mm radial bore clearance, 0.30 mm flange clearance, 0.05 mm rest clearance to the switch and a 0.70 mm inward case stop. The two legs on the removable retainer stop the flange at its inward travel limit. The switch height and printed seating tolerances can exceed the small nominal free play. Seat the daughterboard against the inboard stops, then measure the assembled gap and travel. Correct a preloaded switch by relieving the cap's central underside; select a fitted cap or seat shim for excess free travel. Check continuity open at rest and closed before the shoulder bottoms. The cap must release freely and return to its recess in every orientation before installing the battery.

Check power-on, turn-off, reset/boot service access and the lens edge-touch grid after closing the shell. Repeat while gripping and removing the rod handle. Physical wire dressing, pressure against the closed assembly, pocket actuation, cycle life and retention remain fit-print tests.

## CAD evidence

`Rev-F-top-power.step.gz` contains the populated assembly with the removable rod grip seated. Decompress to STEP for CAD import. For the exposed receiver and dimensioned sections, open [the interface inspection drawing](CAD/grip-interface-inspection.png). The seated and removal poses are in `CAD/`. The housing, cap and retainer STL solids were checked for watertightness. The exterior game-viewer mesh is sampled from the same updated housing and cap geometry.

The power assembly was registered to the Rev E's 546 controller solids, 25 encoder solids, printed bezel, reel hardware, and cell/speaker envelopes. The increased housing material and new fixed parts showed zero interference above 0.002 mm³. The cap was checked at 0.05 mm increments through 0.70 mm travel; expected actuator motion was excluded from rigid collisions. The key board, cap and retainer drops were checked over 22.5 mm at 0.5 mm increments. Final axial cap insertion was sampled every 0.25 mm, and registered main controller closure was sampled at 0.5 mm for candidate pairs. The model represents the switch and trimmed terminal envelopes, without lead-wire shapes or polymer deflection. See `checks.json` and `rod-grip-checks.json`.

`power_key.py` rebuilds the CAD using the registered Rev E sources, CadQuery, NumPy and trimesh. It imports the mating features from `rod_grip.py`. The optical aperture and lens registration remain those documented in the [Rev E fit review](../rev-E/Readme.md). This revision retains the capacitive AXS15231B display.
