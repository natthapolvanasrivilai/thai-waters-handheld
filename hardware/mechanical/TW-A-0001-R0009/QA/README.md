# R0009 engineering verification

## Integrated wiring and service access

The [closed harness audit](dressed-harness.json) checks 54 routing parts against 134 installed parts. It found zero unassigned hard interferences. The [connector and service guide](../../../electronics/harness-r0003/README.md) lists cavity ordering and first-article checks. The [Tensility 10-03675 test](tensility-fit.json) exercised the actual vendor plug in both orientations and eight displaced positions plus nominal per orientation. Minimum rigid housing gap was 0.230 mm; minimum tracked mask gap was 0.35094 mm. The [offset privacy study](offset-privacy.json) still reports peripheral sightlines around the shield at some offsets. Confirm appearance on the actual finish and board lot before cosmetic approval.

## USB access and privacy

The earlier inspection gauge stopped at the mouth of the controller socket at X−40.00 and missed its center by +0.37 mm in Z. It demonstrated zero engagement. In the R0008 section a fully mated, rounded 12.35 × 6.50 mm overmold collided with the old case by as much as 3.79886 mm³. The new case has a continuous shell-colored recess, a guide to the socket and an opaque TPU diaphragm.

At 6.40 mm insertion, a 6.55 mm exposed sleeve leaves the shoulder 0.15 mm in front of the receptacle. The rigid-plastic check uses an 8.28 × 2.43 mm sleeve with conservative 1 mm corner radius and the separate 12.35 × 6.50 mm overmold test body. Nominal overmold clearance is 0.370 mm. With 0.10 mm surface growth and independent ±0.15 mm assembly offsets the overmold retains 0.220 mm. These are defined test profiles. USB-C does not specify a universal cable overmold. Nominal rays are cast from outside over 25 pitch/yaw combinations (0°, ±15°, ±30°) at 0.20 mm target spacing. The original receptacle tongue, shield and mating recess remain visible. ## Three status windows

C is the green stock charger LED, U is the blue USB-voltage-present LED, and P is the red stock enable LED. Each 1.2 mm nominal light guide terminates 0.298–0.380 mm from its actual emitter and clears its nominal case channel by at least 0.0956 mm. Adjacent guide exteriors stay 4.85 mm apart. All 225 modeled rays per window reach its intended emitter. None of the 1,350 wrong-emitter rays clears the opaque housing. These ray counts test channels and crosstalk geometry. Surface finish, emitted intensity, glare, colour contrast and physical transmissivity require a built unit. ## Printing and motion

All 18 single-component STLs, including the two separate inert battery fixtures, have closed, consistently wound, positive-volume meshes and nonzero bed contact. Every elevated downward face is at least 30° above the bed. The revised shell has 264.675 mm² of coplanar bed contact and a 33.393° minimum. The final rotor has a 32.367° minimum. Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61, A1 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm standard, 30° normal auto support, specified outer brims and generic PETG estimated 4.6008 g shell support and 0.4143 g rotor support. Other models used zero support. Generic TPU is used for the mask. Remove support from the housing and rotor before mechanical checks. Numbers are slicer estimates; keep suppliers' actual optical-resin and TPU settings for their chosen processes. [Per-file hashes and toolpaths](print-audit.json)

The battery tests use the catalog body, maximum PKCELL drawing gauge and genuine Adafruit STEP with its PCM. All three clear the installed shell, retainer, speaker and haptics through the open-case insertion corridor. All 16 worst-corner body poses clear the shell and bridge. Minimum nominal cell-to-shell clearance is 0.50 mm and cell-to-bridge 0.65 mm. Battery-to-speaker clearance ranges from 1.67 to 3.17 mm across variants. Status limiter PCB2 and W4 wires lie above the seated battery. Fit the battery before the controller/indicator harness. Disconnect power, free the small USB LED carrier and lift A1/W4 clear before battery removal. The clearance fixture and loose-body envelopes are test tools only. [Battery geometry](battery-fit.json) · [Speaker installation sweep](speaker-fit.json)

The 6,082 mechanism samples cover 0–0.70 mm key motion, 0–5.3 mm screw withdrawal, 0–43 mm handle removal, 0–90° folding and 360° crank rotation at both fold endpoints. Against the R0009 shell additions and exact reel hardware there is no unintended new collision in those samples. Stop-overtravel volumes are 1.608 mm³ for locked backslide, 101.073 for rear lift, 39.855 for over-insertion and 8.614 for screw escape. H2's Ø4.40 mm counterbore clears the Ø4.00 mm maximum head. Cut the lower rotor ear and grip axle M2 × 0.4 threads; the printed files preserve their pilot holes. [Motion results](mechanism.json) · [H2–H5 fit](../../../components/reel-fasteners/fit.json)

The power-key manufacture drill is Ø1.00 ±0.05 mm. Four original PCB CAD holes were Ø1.10. The R0009 carrier now comes directly from the Gerber drill program. Its four unstrained, formed Omron leads each overlap 0.011615 mm³ of board at nominal, with combined overlap 0.087070 / 0.046461 / 0.021213 mm³ at the low / nominal / high drill limits. Physical insertion relies on lead compliance. Nominal cap-to-plunger rest gap is 0.05 mm. Cap motion clears the case (apart from its moving plunger), housing and keeper over the 0.70 mm range checked. ## Physical first article

| Check | Method | Record before production release |
|---|---|---|
| USB seating and direction | Fit the actual controller revision, seal and selected cable. Inspect the plug shoulder and latch engagement, reverse the plug, then charge, enumerate on USB, transfer firmware and reconnect repeatedly. Use the minimum/maximum hole and position coupons as applicable. | Cable SKU, measured shoulder and sleeve dimensions, stop position, both charge/data results and retention. Reject any connector pushed sideways, TPU lip entering the plug path, or cable bottoming on the well. |
| Privacy | With the cable removed, illuminate around the PCB inside the closed unit. Inspect the border of the socket head-on and from all combinations of ±30°. Repeat after cable cycles and after the mask is removed and reseated. | Photographs and any visible path around the original metal receptacle. Inspect the tightest and loosest sample for continuous contact; verify TPU recovery and side loading. |
| Status visibility | Exercise battery-off/on, powered USB with and without charge, completed charge and missing battery, then unplug. Measure VBUS, ESP_EN and charger state independently. Use an operator unfamiliar with the model to identify C/U/P at normal handheld viewing distance under room and bright ambient light. | Correct optically isolated indications and legends; correct recorded charger fault blink when induced on a controlled fixture. Measure brightness/contrast with required material, finishing process and assembled guides. |
| Key and rotating parts | Measure finished PCB holes, insert the switch straight, inspect lead bending and case seating. Solder and inspect joints; pull test on a coupon. Cycle key and verify open at rest, closure, reset and hardware power behavior. Check complete crank and handle travel. | Actual drill, contact/return, solder wicking and lead retention; loaded screw withdrawal/loosening and wear in printed threads. Salt/sweat-test zinc H7 before volume procurement. |
| Battery, speaker and motor | Assemble in prescribed order using an inert battery fixture before a qualified real pack. Dress the production harness, inspect clearances/strain relief and test the speaker and coupled ERM. | Battery/speaker serial or lot, harness strain, screw travel and battery clearance. Verify no rubbing/buzz, no useful-angle dropout with ERM on, and measured drive current/startup. |

## Reproduction

Use CadQuery 2.8, trimesh, NumPy and VTK in the same workspace. The R0008 QA audit_fit.py extracts its frozen Rev G assembly once with --prepare-only. Supply the resulting assembly directory with --cache to audit_port.py, audit_offset_privacy.py, audit_optics.py, audit_assembly.py, audit_haptics.py and audit_key.py. The port check needs --printed housing-battery-bay bezel usb-c-shield-mask. Run check_prints.py with --slicer pointing to Bambu Studio and --work pointing to an empty cache; --reuse accepts only identical mesh, machine, process and filament hashes. The exact-part source notes and physical acceptance evidence remain with the corresponding components.
