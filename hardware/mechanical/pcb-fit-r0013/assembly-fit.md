# Dry assembly of the eight PCB gauges

Use the receiver-down housing from plate 1 and its matching bezel from plate 2 for the complete R0013 trial. Plate 7 carries the eight board gauges plus two long A3 strips and six square A4/A5 spacers. The separate R20 floor sample and keyed carrier let you check the reel sensor on the parts already printed. ## Set up the open housing

Lay the housing display-side up. The reel is at the right (+X), the USB socket at the left (−X), the raised power cap at the top (+Y), and the removable service cover at the bottom (−Y). Installed Z is measured from the CAD datum toward the display. All dimensions below are millimetres. Use an empty, unpowered housing, a caliper and the printed gauges. Keep the two tiny USB boards and the two groups of three square spacers identified as they come off the plate.

1. **A2 reel sensor.** Before fitting the display, lower the sensor gauge into the vacant bay slightly inboard of the right wall. Face the central chip and socket blocks toward the reel and put the row of six small holes along the case floor edge. Move the gauge to the right onto the two diagonal pins. Place four measured 0.25 mm removable shims between the component-side PCB face and the shell's four corner lands. Slotted shim stock can straddle each pin. Keep the central chip and both sockets clear. The board should sit parallel to the wall with the central chip opposite the axle. [Mount geometry](#check-the-a2-mount-on-either-fixture)
2. **A3 haptic board.** Set the two long strips on the open floor, with their 12 mm dimension running top to bottom. The PCB rests on both strips at installed Z4.60, component side up. Point its tall J1 header toward the bottom/service edge. Relative to the PCB's left/bottom corner, the strip centers are (7.20, 10.80) and (15.20, 10.80). The board's right edge is 16.20 mm inboard of the reel's inner wall plane at X47. Its top edge is 3.20 mm above the axle centerline in plan. Verify that it sits flat and that the header clears the display subassembly at closure. The gauge has a solid header and omits its 0.4675 mm underslung pin tails and the removable plug.
3. **Power cap in the flat-floor R0013 case.** Before installing the display, lower the cap inside the top wall with the small pressing face pointing out and the broad flange inside. Start 2.50 mm inboard. Align it with the opening, then slide it outward and check the flange sits in the recess. Remove it after the dry fit. Set the KEY board and west retainer aside for R0015. Their installed positions in the older static model did not verify how the board passes the wall. [Corrected guide](../../../docs/models/R0013-PCB-assembly-corrected.html) · [R0015 board and retainer sequence](../TW-A-0001-R0015/README.md)

4. **USB indicator board.** Set the USB1 gauge aside on this housing. The USB-C well obstructs the top-loading route and the old shelf does not provide a straight inboard slide. Use R0016 plate 1 for the side-entry trial. The printed gauge omits the lamp leads. [R0013 stop in 3D](../../../docs/models/R0013-PCB-assembly-corrected.html) · [R0016 procedure](../TW-A-0001-R0016/README.md)
5. **Inert battery envelope.** Try one plate-6 gauge at a time, broad face on the bay supports, with the wire sockets or stub end toward +Y. Seat the bridge from plate 2 and check the gauge remains free of the bridge and mounting lands. The PKCELL maximum body measures 35.30 × 62.30 × 5.30. The Adafruit gauge has a 37 × 60 body, a 6 mm maximum thickness and 63 mm overall length including stubs. Repeat the final case closure with each gauge. ## Build the underside of the display

Place A1 glass-face down on a clean, soft surface. The solid USB-C shell belongs at the case's left opening. Lay out the three square spacers for each daughterboard on the underside of A1. Put the **plain backs** of A4 and A5 toward A1 and the **component blocks** toward the housing floor. Use the following positions in **display-side plan view** with +X toward the reel and +Y toward the power-key edge. Turning A1 over reverses the apparent left/right direction. The two board edges nearest the reel are flush with each other, 3.01 mm inboard of A1's PCB edge. The gap from the +Y edge of A4 to the −Y edge of A5 is 2.00 mm.

Measure each printed spacer between its bearing faces. The two A3 strips target 2.100 mm. The A4/A5 blocks reproduce the entire dry support stack from the gauge's flat copper-envelope back to A1 at Z21.00. Adding adhesive or another pair of bondline shims changes the spacing. If the printer's layer increments produce a height difference, record the measured value and use calibrated stock of the target height for a nominal-stack trial. The real A4/A5 assemblies use separate insulating spacers and bondlines. [Assembly ledger](../TW-A-0001-R0013/assembly-parts.json) · [A5 bond detail](../TW-A-0001-R0013/QA/A5-bond.json)

Support the daughterboards while turning A1 into place. Removable tabs across exposed board edges may hold the dry stack during handling. Keep them clear of the spacer faces, bezel seam, terminal and nearby boards. If a part shifts, reopen the case and restore its datum before interpreting a closure result.

## Seat the display and inspect the closure

Lower A1 onto its four mounting datums with the glass up and USB-C aligned to the left opening. Check that the lower service switches line up with the removable cover, the key board stays captured and the reel sensor remains on its lands. Bring the matching bezel down evenly by hand. Look for an even seam and daylight around the glass perimeter. The designed glass-to-rim gap is 0.25 mm nominal. Inspect the USB shell against the bezel and the empty shell opening first. The optional plate-5 TPU diaphragm is a compliant part with radial preload. The solid USB gauge permits an external trim check only. Leave the display mounting holes unloaded during this fit check.

Photograph any contact with the part still on its datums. Record which edge touched or rocked, its measured gap, the actual spacer heights and the battery gauge used. Compare closure with the suspect gauge removed to isolate the obstruction. Count out all eight board gauges, eight printed spacers and any temporary shim stock after disassembly. Wires, solder, plug motion, key travel and screw retention have separate checks with their actual parts.

## Check the A2 mount on either fixture

All four A2 holes are nominally Ø2.50 on a 20.32 × 12.70 pattern, in a 1.57 mm board. In installed (Y,Z) coordinates the pinned corners are (+10.16, 21.15) and (−10.16, 8.45). The final gauge mesh measures 2.4996 mm across its pin holes. Record both pin and hole sizes on the physical print.

## Digital checks behind this procedure

The released six-plate X1C baseline carries the same housing (TW-P-0001-R0010) and bezel (TW-P-0006-R0004) listed for R0013. A two-way point-set comparison registers the native 3MF against controlled STL meshes. The maximum centered vertex discrepancy for housing, bezel and both battery gauges is under 0.000003 mm. The pack gauges use their supplier-frame pose translated by (−10.40, −0.65, +3.60) in the case.

The eight final STL gauges were placed using their saved installed transforms. The check compares each gauge with 13 case parts or pack alternatives, all eight dry supports and the other seven boards. The largest nominal shared volume was 0.000035 mm³ at the key board and housing, within the 0.0001 mm³ mesh comparison allowance. Each A3 strip had 36 mm² of supporting area on each face. Every A4 and A5 block had 9 mm² on both faces. The separate R20 carrier shared zero volume with seated A2. Moving A2 sideways by 1.50 mm deliberately produced a 6.28 mm³ intersection. These are nominal digital measurements. Record the as-printed fit separately. [Pairwise measurements and hashes](assembly-fit.json) · [Gauge source geometry and omissions](geometry.json)

To repeat the comparison, run `python check_assembly_fit.py /path/to/Pocket-Reel-R0011-X1C.3mf` in this directory with trimesh, manifold3d, NumPy and SciPy. The three dry-support meshes can be regenerated with `python mesh-shims.py meshes`.
