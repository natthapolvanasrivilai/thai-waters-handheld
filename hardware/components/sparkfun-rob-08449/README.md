# M1 SparkFun ROB-08449

SparkFun Electronics published the original SolidWorks part and neutral STEP under CC BY-SA 4.0. The supplied body, strain relief and wire stubs are used as shipped in the assembly, rotated and translated. Formed wire extension and trim coordinates appear in electronics/haptic-r0009. Attribution and the enclosed license apply to adapted geometry. [Immutable SparkFun source](https://github.com/sparkfun/3D_Models/tree/53d298c3b8202cf8fce9ab61885855720c84c012/products/8449) · [Order ROB-08449](https://www.sparkfun.com/vibration-motor.html)

The catalog tagline says 2 mm. Its linked Zhejiang Yuesui B1034.FL45-00-015 drawing specifies Ø10 × 3.4 mm housing, 3.0 V rated and 60 mA max at rated voltage. Qualify the delivered 08449 variant against the drawing and CAD, then measure ERM startup and operating current with the Boardoza driver before closing the housing.
