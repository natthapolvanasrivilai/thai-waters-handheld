# SP1 · Adafruit 3923

R0009 specifies one Mini Oval Speaker, Adafruit PID **3923**, 8 Ω nominal and 1 W rated. Adafruit lists a 30 × 20 × 5 mm body and a 30 mm factory cable on the post-November-2025 revision. The prototype price reference is USD 1.95 each before shipping, import charges and assembly. Check the purchasing lot and cable revision at receipt. [Manufacturer and ordering](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3923) · [DigiKey 1528-2716-ND](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/adafruit-industries-llc/3923/9658610)

The manufacturer’s 8-solid STEP includes the frame, acoustic face, magnet and both lead stubs. With the stubs the native bounds are 19.90 × 32.50 × 6.50 mm. The route beyond those stubs belongs to the custom harness. Compare polarity at J9 with both bridge outputs floating. Record coil resistance, the actual harness length and fit of the acoustic gasket at incoming inspection. Use a differential dummy-load measurement and listen for rubbing and buzz after closing the case.
