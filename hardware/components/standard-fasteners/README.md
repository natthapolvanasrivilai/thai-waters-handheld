The STEP files are catalogued standard geometry generated by [cq_warehouse](https://github.com/gumyr/cq_warehouse/tree/a57bb7a24762d367d66369f280a24c38a64e73dd) under the included license. They include the helical thread, head recess and nut chamfer. The order references describe A2 stainless parts. Surface roughness in the studio viewer is an artistic material setting. Incoming lots need head diameter/height, thread pitch, and bit engagement checks. The H8 trap requires its flats along the specified orientation.

Run `place.py` to regenerate the installed bodies and `verify.py` to measure them against the matched enclosure CAD. The [intersection results](fit.json) are in mm and mm³. A zero gap with zero intersection denotes nominal contact.
