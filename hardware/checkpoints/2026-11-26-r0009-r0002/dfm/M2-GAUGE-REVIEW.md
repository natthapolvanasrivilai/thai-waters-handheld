Extract the two continuous model paths through the tight side, report the minimum deposited ligament, and keep the housing's separate 12 openings/fastener pilots unaffected.
3. If physical test is later approved, use three coupons at stable bed positions. After cooling and removing loose strings without trimming hole edges, record smallest free GO and largest NO-GO measured pins (into both faces), the actual maximum thread OD of the production M2 screw lot, and observed tight-ligament widths. Keep complete M2 pass on both in-place gauge holes, simultaneous screw pair engagement and mounting-plate seating/rocking as final separate checks; do not force the screw into a gauging hole. Housing engagement/torque is a different test.
4. Prefer the original geometry if measured free-pass is satisfactory. Trial the directional relief only if the entry limits a clear mid-throat. If clear bore throughout is insufficient, the coupon determines print bias to investigate; if sufficient compensation would erode protected web/fit registration, revert and keep the CAD change unapproved. Inert gauge-only post-processing to the original tolerance is a separate candidate, not a fix to the production PCB.

### Simultaneous pair registration screen

Measure both minimum clear openings through the full printed bore, both screw-lot maximum shank/thread diameters, and each bore center residual (X/Y) relative to its seated housing pilot axis with the gauge on its intended datums. Use worst-case measurements including measurement uncertainty. Run `m2_pair_screen.py --diameters BORE_A BORE_B SCREW_A SCREW_B --residuals DX_A DY_A DX_B DY_B`. Each local seated margin is (bore OD minus screw OD)/2 minus that local axis residual magnitude. Both must be positive as a screening precondition. Do not use free translation as acceptance: the PCB opening, datums and connectors can constrain it. The script separately checks that idealized free lateral translation could accommodate the relative center-vector error without claiming post-seating clearance. It is not a rotation, form, loaded or torque model.

The nominal Ø2.30 / Ø2.00 case leaves 0.15 mm radial per bore and <0.30 mm relative-vector accommodation only if entirely free to translate. A common 0.20 mm shift passes the free-translation condition but fails on fixed datums; a 1.70 mm witness cannot prove passage of a 2.00 mm screw. Synthetic controls include unequal openings and failing offset/diameter cases. Record actual simultaneous hand-start/seat/rock, without load or forcing, as a separate gate.
