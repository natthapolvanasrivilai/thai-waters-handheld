# TW-A-0002-R0004 electrical integration candidate

**Engineering hold.** This change specifies a revised A1 controller power entry, load inhibits, bus population, key buffer and speaker attenuation. The physically populated R0003 controller and the R0011 enclosure inspection model do not implement or prove these electronics. A supplier board revision or a placed, routed and qualified interposer is required. The original JST interfaces, switched 3.3 V logic domain, autonomous PMIC charge indication and protected pack connection are design constraints.

**USB-only operation remains open.** With a healthy pack, the proposed input network can limit the source current while the PMIC reduces charging and supplements system demand from the pack. With no pack, a 330 mA startup request cannot be served from a 100 mA host. Three held-load tests fall below the 3.9 V input floor. A separately powered-on-first USB bridge provides a proposed, single-cable recovery path for an empty battery bay. Cold boot, ROM entry and a successful flash still require a physical demonstration. The low-pack response deliberately withholds high-power operation and the audio pad reduces available loudness.

## Measurements and fault disposition

The [exception inventory](exceptions.csv) gives every previously out-of-range numerical reading. Eleven belong to the 42-deck legacy suite and five to the R0003 load suite. The latter includes the 8 Ω RMS limit that its report labeled “bench comparison.” A combined replay adds a seventeenth reading: a nominal blue lamp on the legacy 500 mA trace raises the actual port draw to 500.7656 mA. Five R0003 lamp cases pass their individual screens. Characterization-only motor inrush, heat, temperature input, and panel load remain measurement work. The three bench-brake decks describe an independent fixture with no handheld connection.

## Controller implementation

[Component substitutions and sources](bom-delta.csv) cover the selected parts and identify unpriced control and layout work. Assembly requires a revised board with documented copper and logic. No blind trace cut or connection to an unverified A1 V1/V2 pad is authorized by this specification. Match nets at power-off on the received board, confirm raw/limited VBUS separation by continuity and record the serial, layout, firmware hash and measured stuffing.

1. **Bus.** Place one 1.50 kΩ ±1% resistor on each A1 native 3.3 V line, SDA and SCL, to switched VCC3V3. Remove A3 R1/R2 from the native bus. Keep the A2 shifter bias required on its local side. The tested external-side population is either the central pull-up alone or in parallel with a 10 kΩ ±5% path. Audit A1, A2 and accessories for any other parallel pulls before power-up. Locate and remove surplus pull-ups causing 820 Ω. If the assembled equivalent cannot be reconciled with the bill of materials, reject the assembly. Do not place the central pair on the removable haptic board.
2. **CC and rail.** Each connector CC contact has its own 5.10 kΩ ±1% return to GND. Prove continuity from the actual USB-C contact; R24/R26 designators refer to V2. Keep these returns separate. Select KEMET T520B476M006ATE025, a 3.5 × 2.8 mm, 2 mm-high 47 µF polymer part with 25 mΩ specified ESR. Add C_RAIL directly between VCC3V3 and the MCU ground return, observing polymer polarity. Its installed 30 µF minimum and 40 mΩ maximum include attachment parasitics. Keep the OEM ceramics, regulator feedback routing and compensation intact. Verify added inrush and loop stability. [PMIC electrical characteristics](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/common/X-power-AXP2101_SWcharge_V1.0.pdf)
3. **Key.** The W3 key pulls a local sense gate to ground. Connect Q_KEY_P source to a qualified always-on positive rail present whenever the OEM key is operable. Its 330 kΩ gate-to-source resistor releases it when SW1 opens. Q_KEY_P drain drives Q_KEY_N gate with 100 kΩ to GND. Connect Q_KEY_N source to GND and drain to K3 raw, retaining the stock K3 contacts, R38 and their actual V1/V2 topology. W3 retains its two wires and switch carrier. The active model uses 3.0/4.2 V and conservative transistor threshold/slope equivalents. Validate the chosen always-on rail under USB-only and battery-only states and block reverse power between those domains. [P-channel MOSFET](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PMV65XP.pdf) · [N-channel MOSFET](https://www.nexperia.com/product/PMV16XN)
4. **Speaker.** Put one 4.99 Ω ±1%, 1 W 2512 resistor in series with each BTL leg between the PA and the existing J9 connector. Route the pair together. Both legs float. With at least 9.8802 Ω of series resistance and no more than 5.5 V absolute differential output, the maximum resistive load power is 5.5²/(4 × 9.8802) = 0.76542 W at a matched load. The model also bounds the lowest load in the suite at 0.64004 W per pad resistor. A direct J9 short could dissipate over 1 W per part and needs PA short protection and thermal testing. Actual audio output at the old gain is lower; measure loudness before committing the layout. [Vishay resistor data](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20035/dcrcwe3.pdf)
5. **Weak pack.** Keep J7 directly on the protected cell/charger net. Add C_J7 at the connector, observing polarity, and test connector/protector plug-in surge. Sense J7 with U_UV, using a starting 196 kΩ from J7 to IN+, 105 kΩ to GND and 4.99 MΩ from OUT to IN+. Tie IN− to the internal reference, use local decoupling and keep input leakage low. Qualify actual rising thresholds 3.56–3.67 V and falling thresholds 3.41–3.51 V. Bin or adjust dividers where necessary. Clear a hardware run latch when UV trips and rearm only on a fresh intentional start with valid battery. Its inhibited state must gate the radio/MCU enable, backlight, PA and haptic loads in hardware without disrupting cell charging. The full remaining draw at J7, including the divider, always-on circuitry and capacitor leakage, must stay ≤150 mA. The response case allocates 50 µs from crossing the falling threshold until the heavy current is removed. Verify this time and the inhibited current during a moving load step. With 0.55 Ω and 3.40 V OCV a steady 720 mA would yield only about 3.00 V. A larger capacitor alone cannot meet the steady-state energy balance. [Comparator and reference](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3012.pdf) · [Battery buffer](https://na.industrial.panasonic.com/products/capacitors/polymer-capacitors/series/13072/model/13170)

### USB entry and authorization

Keep the blue branch and J8 raw VBUS takeoff on USB_RAW so the indicator shows connector voltage even when both load paths are off. Separate all PMIC VBUS copper and downstream bulk/load paths from USB_RAW. Ensure J8 exposes no other unaccounted raw-VBUS load. Route the receptacle data pair through U_PAIR. Its native throw feeds BQ24392 DP_CON/DM_CON and that detector's DP_HOST/DM_HOST feeds A1. Its recovery throw feeds the independent bridge. Retain connector-side ESD. Replace R18/R19 with 10 Ω on the native path. Leaving 22 Ω with both new switches gives 22.74 ns in the 100 pF fixture. The routed models use 6 Ω and 7.5 pF for the mux, and 6.9 Ω plus an assumed 8 pF for the detector/route. They use an assumed native driver impedance of 23 Ω and a bridge driver impedance of 17 Ω. Measure the actual high-speed route and programmer connection.

- The weak branch is USB_RAW → opposed P-channel MOSFET pair → R_LOW 56 Ω ±1% → PMIC_VBUS. The pair uses a common-source gate pull-up and an N-channel gate driver. It defaults off, blocks reverse flow and may enable only for an applicable low-current grant. Even a hard short draws at most 5.25/55.44 A through this resistor; rate it for the sustained dissipation.
- The authorized branch is USB_RAW → TPS2553-Q1 IN/OUT → PMIC_VBUS. Fit 88.7 kΩ ±1% from ILIM to GND, local ceramic bypass and an EN pulldown. The manufacturer lists 262.1–342.3 mA production design bounds for this programming resistor. Budget the lamp and all upstream control currents independently. The short-circuit model includes the maximum shunt load of a shorted lamp after its onboard limiter. [TI current-limit table 1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553-q1.pdf)
- With no signal, reset, expired dead-battery timer or host suspend, clear both grants. For a live standard downstream port, enable weak mode only within its grant. Use high mode only after the USB configuration permits at least the required current and while the device remains configured and active. Revoke it on reset, suspend, unconfigure or removal. For a charger, decode BQ24392 outputs and GOOD_BAT by its full Table 1. In particular, CHG_DET alone cannot distinguish full-current authorization from the reduced allowance of a charging downstream port with a dead battery. This part detects charger/data signatures on D+/D−. Qualify C-to-C sources and both orientations without assuming a high-current Type-C grant. Qualify hardwired truth-table logic, fail-safe inputs and always-on domains on the revised A1. The shipped application has no verified GRANT500, suspend or ROM-boot implementation. [TI charger and dead-battery mode table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24392.pdf)

The positive PMIC model uses the published nominal 4.36 V input-voltage regulation setting at ±3% and an incremental input-regulation slope of 10 A/V. The source current fixture preserves the old demand pulse. The excess above the available source is reported as `missing_input` in USB-equivalent amperes. It reaches 0.3879 A at the lossy 650 mA corner. System power must come from a charged pack with converter losses accounted for, or hardware must withhold the corresponding load. Charging current can fall to zero during play. Actual battery current, converter control, source-grant transitions, temperature and startup are outside that surrogate. The 0.75 mA raw-control allowance used for the screens is an aggregate design budget to verify, including status pull-ups, controller quiescent current and leakage. [AXP2101 input regulation](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/common/X-power-AXP2101_SWcharge_V1.0.pdf)

Three retained no-cell controls keep the same 3.9 V minimum. Resistive loads draw 330 or 650 mA at that threshold, with no supplementary source or load shedding. The model gives 0.87524 V before configuration, 2.05380 V under the configured 650 mA request and 0.87524 V after revocation of a grant. These are static overload voltages of a dummy load. Actual silicon may shut down or cycle. Production requires enforced load partitioning and the independent recovery path below. The original overload stays in the evidence as a regression control.

### Single-cable recovery with the battery removed

Use the existing service BOOT control at USB insertion to select recovery. A latch powered directly from USB_RAW samples the switch and holds the selection until unplug. Its controls must work with the ESP erased, off, asleep or missing its supply. In recovery, the TS3USB221A connects the USB-C data pair to an FT231XS-R supplied from USB_RAW. The weak branch, normal charger grant and native controller are held off. A dedicated 3.3 V regulator supplies the mux and selection logic. Use the FTDI reference decoupling, ferrite and 27 Ω per bridge data leg. Power the UART I/O at 3.3 V and isolate both UART directions until the target supply is valid. Measure the total raw-rail capacitance and attach charge across all branches. The pregrant simulation starts after attach; it excludes charging those input capacitors. [USB mux](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ts3usb221a.pdf) · [FT231X bus-powered schematic and MTP defaults](https://ftdichip.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/DS_FT231X.pdf)

| Host state | Hardware behavior and model boundary |
|---|---|
| Before configuration, including a denied 500 mA configuration | Only the recovery bridge, raw blue lamp and control domain draw power. Program a 500 mA bus-powered descriptor and configure CBUS0 as PWREN#. Pull it up to VCCIO with 10 kΩ. The factory 90 mA descriptor is unsuitable. Default-off hardware keeps the target supply and all UART drive into the target isolated. The settled two-corner port maximum is 13.91 mA and the target supply is 0 V. |
| Host grants the requested configuration | Active-low PWREN# enables the current-limited U_INPUT through a default-off buffer. Hold GPIO0 and GPIO46 at the ROM download strap levels; release ESP_EN only after the target supply is stable. Route FT TXD to ESP GPIO44/U0RXD and FT RXD to GPIO43/U0TXD; allow a reset/retry command with a qualified RTS/DTR or physical BOOT/RESET sequence. Hold the display, backlight, PA, SD, haptics, radios and charger inactive. Qualify ESP, flash, regulator and auxiliaries at **≤200 mA total PMIC input** in recovery. In the modeled 200 mA window the worst input is 4.579 V; maximum host demand including the modeled inrush ramp is 277.66 mA. |
| Suspend, USB reset, unconfigure, brownout or detachment during programming | Loss of PWREN# asserts reset, isolates bridge I/O and drops the target grant while leaving the bridge powered for reconnect when VBUS is present. Validate the physical sequence on hardware. The modeled post-revocation active draw is ≤13.91 mA and settled suspend ≤2.16 mA. A partial flash requires full erase/write/verify on the next valid grant. Program the service bridge independently of the target and verify its descriptor and CBUS configuration after manufacture. |

The simulated delay and grant transitions are stimuli. Actual USB enumeration, MCU output current, routed impedance, eFuse ROM policy, device-side wakeup and UART reflash remain open. EC-11 requires an erased sample, no battery, both orientations, multiple hosts, denied/revoked grants, interruption during write and successful full-image verification after retry. The recovery domain uses a 12 mA active and 0.25 mA suspended allowance for its bridge, plus the independent 0.75 mA logic/leakage allocation and the raw blue lamp. [Espressif ROM boot-mode and DTR/RTS circuit](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esptool/en/latest/esp32s3/advanced-topics/boot-mode-selection.html)

## Assembly evaluation and replay

For standalone speaker evaluation, the [audio coupon fabrication package](audio-bench-coupon-fab.zip) contains a 20 × 14 mm two-layer board with four Ø0.8 mm plated holes. Make on 0.8 mm FR4 with 2 oz copper, mask both sides, lead-free finish. Viewed from its component side with layout origin at lower left, x=2 is amplifier input, x=18 is speaker output, y=10 is plus and y=4 is minus. Populate one resistor per row. Verify each end-to-end resistance, absence of cross-row shorts, thermal response and test-fixture strain relief. The coupon is an external bench assembly. Its [source](audio_coupon.py) can regenerate the fabrication files. OEM PCB layout, control BOM completion, mechanical clearance and thermal qualification remain open for the installed product.

Run `python verify.py --engine <path-to-ngspice-33>` from this directory. The [replay tool](verify.py) stages the historical decks in temporary copies, verifies all original scalar values, retains fresh solver logs and checks deck identity. The [R0004 netlists and measurements](corners.py) generate 85 reviewed cases. The [raw archive](solver-evidence.zip) contains each candidate deck and its log plus the fresh logs of all 60 historical decks. The [measurement CSV](results.csv) contains 140 values inside their bounds, 25 descriptive readings and five outside. Three are the unshed no-cell loads above. Two reproduce the previous 500.7656 mA integrated overload and the 22.74 ns data edge with the superseded termination. The runner requires those exact five measurements to exceed unchanged limits. [Engine identity, source hashes and replay inventory](verification.json)

Close the [first-article matrix](first-article.csv) on identified hardware. All eleven gates are open. The altered A1 PCB has no fabrication release or installed STEP. A nonmatching OEM board, unverified programmed thresholds, failed physical connector and unsupplied USB-only workload each require a specific corrective action before prototype release.
