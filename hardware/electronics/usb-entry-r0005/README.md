# R0005 fast-path experiment — rejected

R0005 establishes a real V2-only power disconnect at A1 LRC4 and distinguishes the protected J8-1 tail from the raw J5 receptacle. All seventeen R0004 A5 footprint placements remain fixed. A new ferrite, VBUS TVS and separate lamp wire land fit on the original 23 × 16 mm board. The [component schedule](bom.csv), [schematic](circuit.pdf), [native layout](usb-input.kicad_pcb), [supplier-net check](boundary.json) and [assembly contacts](assembly.csv) describe this experiment.

**Use the [R0006 passive-limit design](../usb-entry-r0006/README.md) for the current engineering candidate.** R0005's TPS2553 path depends on two external control inputs. Neither input has an implemented source in a standalone R0005 handheld. Floating both inputs safely disables PMIC power and charging.

The 42 R0005 decks took 78 measurements. They yielded 56 within limits, nine characterization readings, nine expected fault failures and four failures of intended operating limits. The identical stock bypass gives 348.837 mA. The intercepted 15 Ω fixture gives 75.769 mA with the weak path enabled. A shorted lamp and PMIC give 96.260 mA in weak mode, 344.499 mA on the configured switch and 436.937 mA with both paths enabled. The 3.60 kΩ lamp resistor reduces the pressed-to-ground lamp fault in suspend to 2.223 mA including the aggregate raw-control allowance. Reinstalling the 2.70 kΩ resistor gives 2.714 mA. The 0.3 µH cable and ideal host with a 2 µs or 10 µs delay before the short-current clamp produce 5.465 A and 12.287 A source peaks. These are source-dependent behavioral stresses. With no battery, the 330 mA-at-3.9 V load yields only 0.805 V pregrant and 3.098 V in low-ILIM configured mode.
