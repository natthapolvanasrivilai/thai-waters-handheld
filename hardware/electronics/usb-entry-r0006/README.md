# A5 R0006 USB power entry

**TW-A5-USB-R0006 limits the intentional VBUS branches to 91.289 mA at 5.50 V, even if both pass FETs weld on.** On the same 15 Ω load, the corrected candidate drew 63.529 mA in simulation. The untouched factory control reproduced the original 348.837 mA failure. A positively identified Waveshare 3.5B V2 with LRC4 removed is required. ## Source behavior

| Source | Available operation |
| --- | --- |
| No VBUS, ordinary USB-A to C connection, USB-C default Rp, unconfigured or 500 mA configured legacy host | The PMIC feed stays off. An operating unit runs from its battery. The blue lamp indicates connector power. The default-source suspend fixture stays below 2.5 mA. |
| Non-PD 5 V USB-C source advertising 1.5 A or 3 A on either orientation | A5 enables only the resistor-limited feed. The comparators work from VBUS with the processor off or the pack depleted. Type-C permits continued current under these advertisements during USB suspend. A return to default Rp withdraws the feed. |
| No battery and a 330 mA startup load | The rail misses the 3.9 V input floor. No-cell startup and single-cable flashing remain open. |

At AXP2101 input-regulation voltages of 4.2292–4.4908 V, the worst-drop circuit supplies **3.98–19.71 mA to the PMIC input** across 4.75–5.50 V. At 5.00 V that range is 7.86–11.94 mA. PMIC consumption and losses reduce what reaches the battery. An appropriate C-to-C charger can qualify without an awake processor or healthy pack. Positive battery charge current, charge termination, dead-pack recovery and off-state PMIC behavior need measurement. ## Connections and assembly

1. Identify the A1 lot and stuffing with USB and battery disconnected. The hash-checked V2 supplier schematic lists exactly three pins on VBUS′, J5-A4/B9, J5-A9/B4 and LRC4-1. The protected VBUS net contains LRC4-2, J8-1, D3-1, C20-2, C24-1 and AXP2101 UP1-37. Both OEM 10 µF capacitors belong on this protected side. The supplier STEP contains no LRC4 or BTREF1608A3R121 instance. A matched V2 land photo, physical access and registered land coordinates are outstanding. [Supplier V2 schematic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B/main/hardware/schematic/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B-V2-Schematic.pdf) · [Extracted boundary](boundary.json)
2. On an identified candidate, document both lands before and after LRC4 removal. With A5 disconnected, check breakout VBUS in both orientations to LRC4-1, J8-1 to LRC4-2, and isolation between the two halves in both meter polarities after capacitor settling. Verify separation from VBAT J8-2. Keep the stock ground ferrite LRC5 and both OEM 5.1 kΩ CC terminations. Inaccessible or ambiguous lands hold the article. [Point-to-point schedule](assembly.csv)
3. W1 joins LRC4-1. The ferrite FB1 then feeds exactly R2 and R9. The 62 Ω R2 branch feeds the new D1 TVS and common-source Q1/Q2 pair, returning through W2 to the fixed J8-1 solder tail. The 3.30 kΩ R9 branch feeds CTRL including U1/U2, their capacitors and the blue lamp. W3 goes to the fixed J8-3 ground tail. Remove the previous J8-1 lamp lead completely before connecting W7 to USB-PCB2. Replace the carrier's 2.70 kΩ resistor with 3.60 kΩ and check indicator visibility at the lower current. [Schematic](circuit.pdf) · [Installed wire geometry](installed-leads/README.md)
4. W4 senses R26-2/J5-A5 CC1. W5 senses R24-2/J5-B5 CC2. Each channel has its own 100 kΩ series tap, 10 MΩ pulldown and 10 nF filter. The two TLV3011B comparators share U1's 1.242 V reference through the 100 kΩ/110 kΩ divider. Either elevated CC voltage asserts an open-drain output. Q3 permits the gate pull-down when CTRL is present. The B-version comparators have fail-safe inputs and high-impedance outputs in power-on reset. W6 is an isolated spare pad. Check both physical terminations and orientation-specific results on the actual receptacle. No grant wire or firmware qualification is required. [Comparator pinout and POR](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3011.pdf) · [CC voltage-band reference](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tusb320.pdf)
5. W2, W3 and W7 have installed conductor, insulation and solder geometry. W1 and the two CC taps lack registered V2 land locations. Their solder access, length, joint strain relief, ESD loop and service clearance remain open. Route only after matching the actual lot and support each fine land independently of the removable J8 plug. The raw conductor before the resistors needs insulation throughout. Its deliberate short is retained as a failing control. [Lead-fit scope](installed-leads/README.md)

The passive calculation is 5.50/(62 × 0.99) + 5.50/(3300 × 0.99) = 91.289 mA. Even connecting both resistors directly to ground keeps the intended VBUS branches within that bound. Connector parasitics and CC currents have their own reported quantities. Worst ideal R2 dissipation is 0.493 W. The model uses summed 70 °C routed copper, up to 75 mΩ per candidate fixed lead plus a conservative 75 mΩ return allowance, 0.25/0.65 Ω host loops and near-zero source resistance for stress. It includes both 110 nF CTRL decouplers, comparator/reference current, both CC returns, shorted and normal lamp, and 12 µF each for the two PMIC capacitors, with assumed 40 mΩ/10 nH each. Threshold guards include 1.209–1.274 V reference, ±0.35% divider legs, ±21 mV transfer error and 2 mV modeled hysteresis. The FETs and comparators are behavioral approximations. The attach-window charge and 50 µC screen are engineering measures. [Model and cases](verify_power.py) · [Copper extraction](copper.json)

A permanently asserted comparator can violate suspend while the independent resistor ceiling still holds. A missing OEM Rd under forced-live legacy VBUS can likewise make an over-range sense voltage look elevated. The threshold circuit has no USB-PD or debug-accessory decoder. Measure the response to USB PD signaling, changes in Rp, open CC, hot unplug, brownout and both orientations on the intended source set. Verify actual ESD behavior, resistor temperature, autonomous charging, lamp intensity and no current into the receptacle from a fitted pack. Instrument host current and local capacitor discharge separately. No physical USB compliance, charging or thermal qualification has occurred.
