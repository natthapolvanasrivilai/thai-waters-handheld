# USB-C status and changed loads · TW-A-0002-R0003

Three isolated light guides remain readable when the LCD sleeps. Red reflects ESP_EN through the stock transistor. Blue means voltage is present at the USB input. Green reports the autonomous charger. The handheld schematic, ADC and USB data chain have no added electrical loads from the guides. The new blue branch connects only to the USB rail and ground.

| Condition | Red · power | Blue · USB | Green · charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Powered off on battery | Off | Off | Off |
| Running or asleep with ESP_EN high, on battery | Steady | Off | Off |
| Off, USB powered, charging | Off | Steady | Steady |
| Running/sleeping, USB powered, charging | Steady | Steady | Steady |
| USB present, charging finished, no battery or charge held | Follows ESP_EN | Steady | Off unless charger reports a fault |
| Charger exception while USB is present | Follows ESP_EN | Steady | 1 Hz, 25% on for a charger alarm. 4 Hz, 25% on for source/battery overvoltage. |

The full-charge and missing-cell states share the same collar pattern. Display a measured battery percentage or explicit charger state on the powered screen when available. A connected cable with no voltage has no blue indication. VBUS insertion may wake some PCB/PMIC lots. Red then reflects the actual enable line. A sinking battery or failed supply can extinguish the lamps, so an unlit collar is not proof of electrical isolation. ## Connections and assembly

| Path | Exact connection and construction |
|---|---|
| Green | Stock VSYS → R30 4.7 kΩ → LED1 side-fire green → UP1 CHGLED pin 1. Couple optically. |
| Red | Stock VSYS → R31 4.7 kΩ → LED2 side-fire red → T2 BSS138 → ground. T2 gate follows ESP_EN via R32 1 kΩ. Couple optically. |
| Blue | A1 J8 VBUS exposed inner-row solder tail → W4A short red → USB-PCB2 input PTH → USB-R1 2.70 kΩ → USB-PCB2 output → W4B red → USB-PCB1 A2/anode → blue LED → K1/cathode → W4C black → verified J8 ground tail. No tapping of CC/data lines, CHGLED, VBAT, 3V3, display power, or connector shell. |

The two 3.5B schematic revisions show pin 1 of J8 on VBUS and pin 2 on VBAT. The connector-face illustration labels the 5 V side “2” and BAT “1.” Use the **net and physical solder fillet**. Disconnect the battery and USB, and continuity-identify the VBUS takeoff to an unpowered USB-C breakout's VBUS contact through the board input path. Identify the ground tail to board ground. Check isolation from VBAT with a meter, allowing for PMIC junctions/capacitor charging. Before soldering, install a current-limited 5 V USB source with the battery removed to independently identify VBUS. Disconnect again. Photograph the measured land on the actual serial-numbered board. The stock SMT J8 presents exposed gull-wing tails. The planned selected inner row has VBUS at case x−19.06 and GND at x−16.52, y19.82, z20.75. Reject any lot for which these positions do not match the measured nets; revise the routing for that lot. 1. Panelize the two original 0.6 mm FR4 carriers and support them on a carrier pallet during soldering. Use two-layer 1 oz copper, plated Ø0.40 mm finished holes, ENIG or lead-free HASL, soldermask both faces and the supplied outline. Ask the fabricator to add panel rails and tooling outside the finished outlines. Confirm depaneling leaves the dimensions specified in the layout. [LED carrier artwork](USB-blue-fab.zip) · [Current limiter artwork](USB-limit-fab.zip) · [Pad coordinates and drill](carrier-layout.json)
2. The LED pad pattern has 0.80 mm square lands with 0.85 mm between edges per Kingbright. On the component-face drawing, the left pad is cathode (1) and right pad anode (2). Confirm the underside polarity mark and the assembled diode in diode mode. USB-PCB2 is nonpolar. With the resistor and LED connected, measure 2.70 kΩ ±1% across PCB2 and electrically verify the complete circuit on the bench.
3. Form each wire against its own route and trim to its solder tail. The blue carrier drops into the keyed slot with the lens facing west (case −X). Its pad axes run along Y. The source-side resistor carrier sits near J8 at x−21, y18 and z17.6. Use USB-BOND, a shaped DOWSIL 3145 gray RTV fillet bridging the source carrier to the verified molded side wall of J8 at Y20.39. Mask the mating openings and solder joints. Position the PCB in a removable fixture, wet the clean board underside and header wall along the approved dispense envelope, cure to supplier instructions through the full bead thickness, then insulate the exposed top solder lands. Inspect under magnification after curing. Fixture-screen a 1 N load in three directions for 10 s per direction, then 30 case open/close cycles and vibration with zero detachment, conductor strain or altered continuity. Qualify adhesive adhesion to the actual J8 housing resin and FR4 before assembly release. Keep its under-insulation takeoff short. The solid prototype wires require strain relief at both ends and clearance during case opening. Neither solder joints nor the LED lens serve as supports. Verify the fixed board retention and wire insulation on the physical build. The case optics, local power/charger wells and supply-side lamp require three separate channels. 4. Receivers must confirm the purchased supplier-board lot implements the expected subcircuit. MCU startup selects Type A and checks register 0x69[2:0] = 001. The PMIC handles steady and fault indications after that setup with the processor asleep or off. Mode bits reset on actual removal of all PMIC power to an eFuse-defined default. Test a never-booted unit with a cell emulator/USB supply. Qualify only Type-A-by-default lots if the complete green convention is required before first MCU boot after full power removal. The hardware-only blue signal remains independently valid. ## Cost and circuit evidence

Added electrical material allocation is **USD 1.20 per unit** at the listed assumptions. The full prototype BOM allocates insulating tape to C5. This electrical subtotal excludes the shared C5 tape allowance, optical material, labor, freight, tax and board-panel setup/minimums. The two LEDs and their drivers already populated on A1 add zero dollars. The internal rework uses one new emitter, one resistor, two custom carriers and three cut wire segments. [BOM and sources](bom-delta.csv)

Five actual ngspice 33 DC cases use a piecewise LED knee, 10 Ω forward slope and the ±1% resistor corners. The assumed effective LED knees of 2.15, 2.8 and 4.0 V bound load and light sensitivity. The supplier quotes forward voltage at 20 mA, while this branch operates near 1 mA. The prototype must pass the optical test at its installed current.

| Case | Added USB current | Resistor dissipation |
|---|---:|---:|
| USB absent, disconnected branch | 0 mA in model | 0 mW |
| 5.0 V nominal | 0.812 mA | 1.78 mW |
| 4.4 V, high assumed LED knee | 0.146 mA | 0.058 mW |
| 5.25 V, bright corner | 1.155 mA | 3.57 mW |
| 5.25 V, short **downstream** of R1 | 1.964 mA | 10.31 mW |

There is no intentional cell-powered path for the blue branch. Unplugged leakage/backfeed on the populated A1/PMIC, unintended upstream shorts, active/disconnected USB suspend and total input-current compliance require measurements. A 100 mW resistor leaves dissipation margin within these simulated conditions. A misplaced takeoff on VBAT would destroy the intended indication and introduce standby drain. ## R0003 audio, haptics and shared bus

The Boardoza DRV2605L board J1-1 takes the switched 3.3 V bus, J1-2 IN/TRIG is tied locally to J1-5 ground in the ROM-only configuration, J1-3 is SCL (GPIO7), J1-4 SDA (GPIO8), J1-5 ground. TP1 OUT+ and TP2 OUT− drive SparkFun ROB-08449 across its two leads. Use continuity and pad designators on the actual board. The IC enables from VCC, uses 7-bit address 0x5A and shares the encoder's 3.3 V logic domain. Carry the two motor outputs as a pair and keep them away from the AS5600/magnet. Transfer by net name when reworking the legacy Adafruit driver harness. Boardoza R1 and R2 each pull SDA/SCL high through 2.2 kΩ. [Boardoza published KiCad board and schematic](https://github.com/Boardoza/Boardoza_DRV2605L_Haptic_Driver_Breakout_Board) · [TI driver datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv2605l.pdf)

Firmware sets register 0x17 to 138 in ERM open-loop ROM mode. TI equation 6 predicts 2.979 V average at full scale. The motor is rated 3 V, its published operating range is 2.3–3.6 V and its specified running current maximum is 60 mA. A 33 Ω running example with an assumed 1 V back EMF yields 60.0 mA. Zero-back-EMF startup over 26.4–78 Ω predicts 38.2–112.9 mA before PA/rail resistance. Sample inductances are assumptions. Clip a differential probe and current shunt at M1, test cold start, hot windings, all effects, rapid detents, concurrent radio and direction reversal. Compare the filtered differential envelope, raw PWM peaks, rail droop, current, temperature and minimum perceived click. A programmed average exposes no instantaneous overvoltage or stall guarantee. [SparkFun ROB-08449](https://www.sparkfun.com/vibration-motor.html) · [Firmware clamp](../../../firmware/WillowCreek/Rumble.cpp)

On the 400 kHz I²C bus, R1/R2 of A3 in parallel with an assumed 10 kΩ A2 path form 1.803 kΩ per line. Simulated rise time is 153 ns at 100 pF but **306 ns at 200 pF**, exceeding the 300 ns Fast-mode ceiling. An additional 4.7 kΩ pull-up reduces that 200 pF case to 221 ns and requires 2.23 mA sink at 0.4 V. A measured effective 820 Ω would demand 3.54 mA at 0.4 V. Check each line on V1/V2 with all populated boards and harnesses. Accept measured rise ≤300 ns and VOL ≤0.4 V with weakest attached target; resolve the actual stuffing before choosing the population. [I²C specification](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/user-guide/UM10204.pdf)

SP1 changes to the Adafruit 3923 8 Ω / 1 W speaker. Keep NS4150B J9's two differential outputs and the existing J9 mating plug from the Waveshare speaker. Prepare a verified, insulated two-wire termination to SP1. Its supplied plug cannot be presumed to mate J9. Preserve electrical polarity by tracing each lead, confirm no terminal-to-ground continuity, and perform a pull and rattle test. Switch power off and disconnect the cell before rework. Use an 8 Ω power dummy load and an isolated or suitable differential scope probe across J9. Class-D carriers require an audio-band low-pass measurement for program RMS. Set a **2.53 Vrms / 0.80 W** sustained design limit into 8 Ω. Model examples at 3.30 and 3.60 V sine peak give 0.681 and 0.810 W respectively. A sensitivity case of 6.4 Ω reaches 0.851 W at 3.30 V peak. Characterize actual impedance vs frequency, crest, music + clicks + cues at maximum settings, clipping, acoustic level and coil temperature. Calibrate the firmware gain against these measurements before release.
