# A5 USB input current control

The [KiCad project](usb-input.kicad_pro) routes a 23 × 16 × 0.8 mm two-layer power daughterboard. W1 receives raw connector VBUS, W2 feeds the PMIC and W3 is the return. The R2 path limits default-source current. U1 supplies the configured path. W4 and W5 require externally qualified enables and W6 is the open-drain fault output. [Circuit drawing](circuit.pdf) · [Wire schedule](harness.csv) · [Orderable components](bom.csv) · [Placements and pin map](design.json)

The reference controller design requires a revision-controlled A1 supplier spin or proven isolation at the received controller. Stock A1 VBUS bypasses A5. The replay's bypass control drew 348.837 mA against the 100 mA fixture limit. The identities of the A1 tap pads, grant/inhibit circuit, six installed wires, fault pull-up, transceiver isolation, no-cell behavior and USB differential routes remain open. A5 and A4 provide partial implementations of the larger controller ECO. [Controller implementation gate](../integration-r0004/implementation-gate.md)

KiCad 10.0.8 returned zero ERC, DRC, unconnected and schematic-parity issues on this board. Independent copper/drill reconstruction matched ten nets and 35 pads with 20 plated holes. Nominal plotted minimum copper spacing was 0.25132 mm and copper-to-edge was 0.70 mm. The [review archive](usb-input-fab.zip) is engineering evidence. Inspect actual component derating, FAULT_N timing, thermal rise around the 56 Ω resistor, and controller net isolation on a qualified article before use.
