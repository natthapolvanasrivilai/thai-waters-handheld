# Test evidence and market readiness

[Handbook contents](Home.md) · [Repository home](../../README.md) · [Interactive handbook download](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/releases/download/design-review-r0009-1/Thai-Waters-handbook.html)

Use **Test plan** to filter by engineering validation (EVT), design validation (DVT) or production validation (PVT). Expand a test to identify the fixture, input condition, acceptance evidence and outstanding action. Review each open item against the SKU that will actually ship.

- [Verification matrix](../../manufacturing/rev-F-review/verification-matrix.csv) and [risk register](../../manufacturing/rev-F-review/risk-register.csv) track disposition.
- [Architecture and interfaces](../../manufacturing/rev-F-review/architecture.csv) ties hardware and firmware to evidence.
- [Market review](../../compliance/market-access.md) and [jurisdiction register](../../manufacturing/rev-F-review/market-access.csv) track the US, EU and Thailand assessments, including FCC, CE, NBTC and TISI/มอก applicability.
- [Lifecycle gates](../../manufacturing/gates.md) distinguish a fit sample, a design-validation build and a released SKU.

Market classification, supplier declarations, completed-unit tests and lab evidence remain open. Keep the radio module identification, host antenna placement, intended age range, final packaging, battery transport documents, importer identity and firmware version with the applicable market file.
