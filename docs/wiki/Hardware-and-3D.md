# Inspect the assembly and battery pocket

[Handbook contents](Home.md) · [Repository home](../../README.md) · [Interactive handbook download](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/releases/download/rev-g-fit-print/Riffle-Rev-G-handbook.html)

Choose **3D assembly** in the handbook. Drag to orbit. Select a wire, screw or board to see its part record, isolate a group, move the explode slider and step through the seven installation stages. Use the section plane at the battery fences and the reel sensor. The detachable grip uses a 35 mm slide with captive M3 retention. The top-left power cap operates a separate switch carrier.

The Rev G viewer has 86 selectable records. Select **Battery bay** and change the cell option to inspect its actual wire routes, mounting lands, lift-out bridge, relocated PH adapter and the west-side key keeper. Both maximum envelopes cleared 2,486 relevant component pairs per configuration and their open-shell insertion paths. Check the two inert gauges in the print before fitting a real pack.
