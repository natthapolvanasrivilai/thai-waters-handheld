# Inspect the assembly and battery pocket

[Handbook contents](Home.md) · [Repository home](../../README.md) · [Interactive handbook download](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/releases/download/rev-f-engineering-0.7/Riffle-Rev-F-engineering-review.html)

Choose **3D assembly** in the handbook. Drag to orbit. Select a wire, screw or board to see its part record, isolate a group, move the explode slider and step through the seven installation stages. Use the section plane at the battery fences and the reel sensor. The detachable grip uses a 35 mm slide with captive M3 retention. The top-left power cap operates a separate switch carrier.

The 79 selectable records include 16 proposed wire runs. The catalog-envelope test examined 2,109 pairs. The product-linked battery drawing intersects the housing by 11.983 mm³ at nominal and 30.124 mm³ at maximum. The alternate supplier solid overlaps it by 118.397 mm³. Print both **inert** gauges and measure the received pack, cap, plug and lead exit before any powered closure. A working solution also needs clearance for the two plug halves.
