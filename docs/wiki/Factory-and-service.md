Trial the USB indicator with the unpowered R0016 case and the leadless Plate 7 gauge. On earlier housings, skip this gauge. Record shelf seating, free withdrawal and support cleanup. [Fit procedure](../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0016/README.md)

For the R0015 key, place the cap from inside, then lower the board and west retainer. Reserve the older cases for a cap-only check. [Step-by-step key fit](../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0015/README.md)

For the R0014 dry fit, run a fingertip along both A3 seats to check for strings, set the unpowered board gauge directly on the hard lands, and check for rocking before seating the display. Use both long strips only with an older flat-floor R0013 shell. [R0014 assembly instructions](../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0014/README.md)

Select **Assembly process** for the ten factory stations and the downloadable unit record. In the 3D viewer, step through the mechanical order to inspect access before routing a harness.

1. Identify the incoming module, pack, printed shell, fasteners and option set. Measure the inert pack gauge and check the two connector bodies before fitting electronics.
2. Inspect the bezel, power key, reel and sensor air gap. For Rev G, use the [west-side key keeper and lift-out wire bridge](../../hardware/mechanical/rev-G/README.md). Dress the two 100 mm leads and secure the PH adapter to its saddle. Verify polarity, isolation and reel travel with the unit open.
3. Program against the release digest. Record the touch grid, motion, signed turn count, sound, rumble and current on the serialized traveler.
4. Release a closed sample only after its open items clear. Record failures, rework, final checks and the approved lot configuration.

Record the exact enclosure and electrical integration IDs with the serial. For rework, retain the original IDs and record the installed revisions, affected parts, retest and disposition. The historical labels resolve through the [hardware revision register](../../hardware/revisions/README.md).
