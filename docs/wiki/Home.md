# Thai Waters product wiki

[Handbook contents](Home.md) · [Repository home](../../README.md) · [Interactive handbook download](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/releases/download/firmware-v0.8/Thai-Waters-0.8-handbook.html)

This handbook follows a unit from a cast on the simulator through component selection, enclosure fit, programming, factory acceptance and sale. Start with the activity below, or [download the interactive handbook](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/releases/download/firmware-v0.8/Thai-Waters-0.8-handbook.html). Open the downloaded HTML in a browser. The playable game, 3D meshes, circuit traces and costing data are embedded. A local copy is also available at [docs/index.html](../index.html) in your checkout.

**Build disposition · TW-A-0001-R0008 is the next inert fit print.** The print set preserves both maximum battery envelopes. Follow the [R0008 orientation, support and gauge procedure](../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0008/PRINT-GUIDE.md) before powered closure. The rail transient, shared I²C bus, speaker drive and charger behavior have outstanding measurements. Check [electrical verification](Electrical-verification.md) and [mechanical fit](Hardware-and-3D.md) before closing a powered case.
