# Prototype baseline
The player casts with rod motion and reels a right-side folding handle. A landscape 480 × 320 display occupies the front. Keep USB data, the bootloader recovery path, sound configuration, accessible connectors, and the option to fit haptics.

Rev E stows at 108.5 × 69 × 30 mm. Phase 1 uses Waveshare 31137, Adafruit 6357 and a protected 1200 mAh cell. Clock, radio, sensor access and signed firmware releases are software requirements. Verified part evidence, an actual fit print, battery measurements, release keys and supplier-controlled drawings are needed for a production baseline.

The initial market review covers Thailand, the US and the EU. The manufacturer of record, age grade, sales channels, launch countries and bundled accessories are open owner decisions. Record choices against the physical variant and software version.
