# Prototype baseline
The player casts with rod motion and reels a right-side folding crank. The front display must support bare-finger capacitive touch in landscape at 480 × 320. Fishing spots, the collection, tackle and settings must be operable on the display. The Rev F design places a guarded physical power key on the top edge toward the player's left, reachable with the screen facing the player. A removable rod grip supports casting gestures and leaves the handheld pocketable when detached.

The approved prototype A1 is Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B, SKU 31137, with its integrated 3.5-inch capacitive IPS panel and AXS15231B controller. The stock flex carries display and touch signals. Incoming inspection records the PCB revision and verifies single-finger touch through the production-intent opening. Requalify changed modules, glass, overlays or bezel geometry with the same touch test. [Waveshare specifications](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B)

The removable rod grip must seat on rigid load-bearing rails against an end stop and lock against withdrawal with a captive fastener in metal threads. The mounting interface must resist rocking in both casting directions, remain inspectable, and allow operation of the reel, screen and power key with the grip installed. Record tip play, applied load, residual displacement and attachment cycles on the assembled prototype before assigning a service load rating.

Rev E stows at 108.5 × 69 × 30 mm. Phase 1 uses Waveshare 31137, Adafruit 6357 and a protected 1200 mAh cell. Clock, radio, sensor access and signed firmware releases are software requirements. Verified part evidence, an actual fit print, battery measurements, release keys and supplier-controlled drawings are needed for a production baseline.

The initial market review covers Thailand, the US and the EU. The manufacturer of record, age grade, sales channels, launch countries and bundled accessories are open owner decisions. Record choices against the physical variant and software version.

Use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all player-facing text on the web simulator and on the LCD. Opening a player menu keeps the current fishing location visible and pauses fishing timers. The Rev F rear receiver adds 3.2 mm to the 30 mm stowed depth. Every shell carries its receiver nut before closure.
