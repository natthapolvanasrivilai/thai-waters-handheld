---
name: thai-waters-hardware-verification
description: Change the physical Thai Waters handheld, select prototype components or verify mechanical and electrical revisions.
---

Start with the [revision register](../../../hardware/revisions/) and the current assembly's [hardware handbook](../../../docs/wiki/Hardware-and-3D.md). Use the controlled numeric revision for new work and retain legacy labels for traceability. Confirm purchased part number, vendor CAD, source dimensions and maximum envelope before declaring fit. Identify unresolved model or tolerance gaps.

For service motion, identify the fixed loom and anchors, moving plug and contacts, flexible branch, and removable service parts. Register the mating datums on the manufacturer geometry. Inspect the catch, pins and contact bores in section. Record simplifications and supplier-model intersections. Model unlatching before translation and verify the anchors throughout the stroke. Give the limiting pair, phase, travel, units, tolerance assumptions and interval bound or sampling uncertainty. Keep a negative control and give the viewer the same motion and numeric evidence used by the checker. A dimensioned path does not measure latch force or jacket fatigue.

For service with a seated display, keep the display, its fixed leads and every loom anchor installed in each test. Check the flexible lead against the actual terminal lock as well as adjacent wires and both battery variants. Tie each motion report to the PCB population and cable manifest it exercised. If a supply failure changes the board population, repeat the installed check using the new geometry.

For first-print failures, identify the machine, project, plate, material, orientation and failed feature from the owner's evidence. Compare against the exact as-sliced baseline. Keep fixture or coupon revisions distinct from assembly IDs, verify how the coupon transfers to the product, and require a repeat print before claiming strength or easy support removal.

Check the battery at its maximum specified dimensions, wire exits, bend space, connector access, screws, reel deployment, grip retention and power key in the assembled and exploded views. Check build orientation, teardrop horizontal bores, underside angles, clearance and support needs against the actual print configuration. Link fit results to the print files they exercised.

For electrical changes, start from [electrical verification](../../../docs/wiki/Electrical-verification.md). Retain the circuit deck, component model assumptions, test corners, limits and results with the revision. Distinguish simulation from measured bench performance. Update impacted BOM rows, assembly steps and [validation evidence](../../../docs/wiki/Validation-and-compliance.md) when a change alters them.

For wired assemblies, validate installed cable solids through the mating contact, document cavity orientation and distinguish a nominal solder joint from unintended interference. Inspect the closed case, adjacent loom and service stroke. Publish the CAD clearance report alongside the [harness assembly instructions](../../../hardware/electronics/harness-r0003/README.md). Price the exact cable and terminated pigtails shown.

For a closed-case opening, identify its function on the assembled STEP and measure both wall faces. Check closure over the full aperture and at allowed fit offsets. Verify snap retention, neighboring components, open service-tool travel and any separate acoustic path. Render the installed and removed part from the same exterior view and slice the exact released mesh. The [lower-edge service cover](../../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0010/README.md) records the procedure and physical fit checks.

For a changed shell, inspect every exterior side in installed coordinates, including the hidden underside. Before changing CAD, declare the nominal plane, signed outward axis, reviewed footprints and minimum continuous inboard stock from the controlled drawing or an independently checked reference. Name and source each intended opening and each legitimate protrusion separately; cap protrusion height and do not widen a mask just to pass. Include locations of displaced or removed internal features, even when the envelope is unchanged.

Then run the [nominal flat-exterior gate](flat_exterior.py) on the exact candidate STEP/BREP. Check both unexpected material beyond the plane and missing stock inside it. A clean exterior render, unchanged global bounds, and a warning-free native slice do not prove either. Run the previous failing part as a negative control where available. Record part and region-file digests, exact scope and exceptions, added and missing volumes, deepest protrusion and a matching exterior/section image. Use a regional mask only for explicitly reviewed features; do not report unmeasured areas as clear. Missing data, invalid geometry, an unverified exclusion or a failed numerical check holds the claim. Once nominal CAD passes, reopen and slice the exact print mesh and retain its digest and a physical flatness or fit check separately.

Run locally where CadQuery/OpenCascade is available: `python .agents/skills/thai-waters-hardware-verification/flat_exterior.py PART.step REGIONS.json`. The JSON root is `{"regions": [...]}`. Each named region defines `axis` (x/y/z), `plane_mm`, `outward` (-1/+1), `footprint_mm` (two coordinate intervals in remaining XYZ order), and `stock_depth_mm`. Optional `allowed_protrusions` and `stock_openings` entries require a name, a reason/source and their own footprint; the former also requires `max_height_mm`. Zero from the command means scoped nominal CAD pass only; exit 1 is a geometric failure, 2 is unverified input/tooling. Never skip the measured gate because this helper cannot represent a curved or oblique face: use its appropriate exact CAD equivalent.

For load-bearing printed rails, inspect the released toolpath at the first model layer and at every start supported solely by support material. Record nozzle, layer, material, first-layer connected area, longest elevated start, support mass and removal access. A physical force and wear record is needed to qualify the rail.

For crimped interfaces, identify header, plug and individual contact by exact part number. Check stripped conductor and insulation against the crimp specification, cavity by cavity. Verify seating depth, wire exit, mating stroke and tool access. Preserve original failed electrical results and intentional fault injections when recording a candidate circuit correction. Treat preboot behavior and physical measurements as separate evidence.

For a captive cap or guided board, verify the entire motion from above the rim to its seat, then sweep the next board and retainer with the cap held in place. Include the real switch rim, terminals and printable gauge. Keep a rejected path as a regression check. Drive each viewer step from its revision’s measured route and mark obstructed operations on older prints. [Power-key example](../../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0015/README.md)

For an indicator behind a socket, check its route from outside the open housing and compare the native deposited paths through the passage with the gauge cross-section. Show the overlying housing in section. Compute the cable-length bound to the other board before applying any gauge sequence to a soldered lamp. [USB indicator example](../../../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0016/README.md)

For a guided dry assembly, derive both the geometry and final positions from the verified fit kit. Keep alternative fixtures and battery gauges mutually exclusive. For hidden interfaces, show the component face before seating it and show the support positions separately. Move attached parts together when demonstrating a display assembly. Label illustrative shim stock and distinguish a fit check from a physical result. [Plate 7 assembly sources](../../../docs/handbook/pcb-assembly/build_scene.py)

When incorporating loose PCB supports into a housing, carry the installed PCB datum and real pin-tail clearances into the solid model. Verify continuous material into the floor and deposited toolpaths across each seat. Where the original material provided adhesion as well as height, keep hard seating and bond retention as distinct acceptance tests. Mark which loose gauges an existing print owner can reuse.
