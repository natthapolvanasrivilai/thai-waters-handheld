# Thai Waters phone playtest

Open the [private Pocket Reel playtest](https://thai-waters-pocket-angler.shoung0690.chatgpt.site) in your phone browser and sign in with the ChatGPT account that owns the Site. The [phone QR](phone-qr.png) opens the handbook on the same HTTPS site; choose **Open playtest**. The game runs over Wi-Fi or cellular. For a standalone offline build, check its source and checksum using [software verification and offline limits](../../docs/wiki/Software-and-tests.md#current-release). Older engineering downloads and the game embedded in the archived hardware handbook are not the current hosted game.

1. On the Pocket Reel title, tap **Touch to start** (**แตะเพื่อเริ่ม**) to open the main menu. To change language before playing, open **Settings** (**ตั้งค่า**) and choose **English** or **ไทย**; the choice is saved in this browser. Return to the main menu and choose **Play** for a new game or **Continue** for a saved game. If a catch is pending, choose **Resume pending catch**. On a phone, rotate to landscape for a wider screen. The LCD header can request browser fullscreen where supported. Choose **Device view** to try the simulated Rev F controls.
2. Travel through **Menu → Fishing spots → Fish here**. Changing waters plays a first-person journey by footpath, hiking trail, road or longtail boat. **Skip** goes directly to the fishing view. Open **Menu → Rod workshop** and choose a technique, bait and depth. Float drift asks you to distinguish a nibble from a bite. The spoon needs a short wind followed by a pause. The jig needs a lift followed by a drop. Each technique has on-screen cues.
3. Tap the water where you want the float to land. The center channel is clear. Pause and lift to free a snagged branch, or lower the rod and use **Pay out** at a rock. Reel in debris to identify it. For a fish, tap to strike, then drag around the larger lower-right reel clockwise to retrieve in screen-only view. In Device view, drag the folding crank around its axle. When the fish runs, ease off or pay out. When it swims toward you, take up slack quickly. A short cue marks the landing. Its melody identifies rarity and its ending identifies the reward. The fish stays in view until you choose **Save catch & keep fishing** (or the Thai equivalent) on the catch screen.
4. For rod motion, open **Menu → Motion bench → Enable motion** in the phone browser and grant access. Hold the phone comfortably until **Centered** appears, then resume. Lift about 20° and return to the starting angle in one smooth sweep to cast. The first lift teaches the game your grip direction. Rotating between portrait and landscape starts centering again. **Recenter** and **Set custom lift** are available for a different grip or range. The bench shows the incoming sensor source and live rod angle. If a preview blocks sensor access, open the private HTTPS link directly in your browser.
   To wind the reel, trace the larger touch reel's rim or the 3D grip with one finger. In screen-only view you can also hold the reel's center for steady retrieval. The game suppresses stray pointer menus on gameplay controls while leaving text editing and keyboard controls available. For hands-free winding, turn on **Wrist reel** in Motion bench. Lift once to teach the rod direction. While working a spoon or fighting a catch, keep the rod low, twist the phone sideways about 25°, then return near center. Each outward stroke winds half a turn. Straight lifts continue to control the rod. The bench shows the live roll angle, accepted turns and sensor sample count.
5. To try a hosted shared event, open the private Site in two separate browser profiles or on two phones signed into the owner account. Choose the same fishing spot and join **Menu → Events & rankings** in each. The goal is eight catches per three-minute round.
6. Tap **Enable sound** over the water for a two-note check. Open **Menu → Music & bite cues → Test sound** to replay it or recover after switching apps. The iPhone uses media playback volume and the currently selected audio output.
7. In iPhone Safari, tapping **Sound with bite cues** changes the setting and, on iOS 18 or later with system haptics enabled, can produce a native switch tick. Safari exposes no timed vibration for fish fights. **Prawn pick**, **Bottom hold** and **Fast run** audition their rhythms through sound and light. On vibration-capable Android browsers, **Test phone vibration** requests two pulses and the rod rumble control sets the level. Explicit Off persists.
8. The tackle box, collection, rod workshop and settings open over the right side of the fishing location. The water and rod continue animating while your catch clock pauses.

9. Open **Menu → Rod workshop → Career & backups → Export backup** before changing devices or clearing browser data. Save the file somewhere you can find it. On the other browser, choose the file, review the preview, then tap **Replace career from backup** only if you want to overwrite that browser's current career. This is a manual file transfer, not cloud sync.

The collection, rod setup and settings are saved in the browser on each device. A real handheld uses its own save. The hosted browser event and the physical prototype’s local-network event host run separate rooms. The segmented battery on the phone is simulated and can be changed in Motion bench. The web preview does not verify physical handheld fit, reel behavior, or electrical safety.

The daily specimen cup names one water, a species and a minimum length. Enter before landing your catch. Tap the specimen to file it before midnight Bangkok time. Longest fish wins, equal lengths share a rank, and each angler keeps one personal best. Hosted phone sessions share the private site board. LAN browsers and physical handhelds share the LAN host board. Session cookies identify browser anglers; deleting cookies creates a new angler. Rankings are for prototype playtests and use client-reported catches.

Use the Rod workshop to choose hook bait and fishing depth. In the Fish collection, search a name or use the Fictional, Mythic, Collected and This water filters. Open a fish detail and swipe left or right to move through the collection; **Back** still returns to the list. Each entry gives a preferred bait and depth. To try the optional five-portrait pilot on a compatible browser build, add `?fishArt=hq` to the game URL. Default artwork and the physical controller are unchanged. At the Chao Phraya, a bottom hook tipped with raw shrimp can tempt the giant river prawn at dusk or night. It cannot take a surface presentation. Changing destination plays a short arrival animation; a tap skips it and the next tap casts.

In Thai browser mode, *Taeniura lymma* (specimen 1433) is labeled **ปลากระเบนทอง**. After catching it, you can also search the Fish collection for **ปลากระเบนหางพัดจุดฟ้า**. [Thai label source](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/blob/90d5c37e162c118df6563f7836f41cff363edab8/game/content/thai-specimen-labels.md#rayong-pilot-label-50).

The browser defaults to Thai. The title menu’s **Settings → Language** (**ตั้งค่า → ภาษา**) is available before fishing. On landing a fish, read its card at your own pace, then choose the on-screen save/continue action to file it and resume fishing. Open the collection to revisit it. In the rod workshop, each bait has an illustrated preview and a matching thumbnail in the bait picker.

Tap a spot on the water to aim and cast. The tackle icons under the location open the Rod workshop. During a fight, read line tension just above the moving splash.
