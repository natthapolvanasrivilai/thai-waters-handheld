# Thai Waters phone playtest

Open [Thai Waters](https://thai-waters-pocket-angler.shoung0690.chatgpt.site) in your phone browser and sign in with the ChatGPT account that owns the private Site. The QR alongside this guide opens the same HTTPS address. The game runs over Wi-Fi or cellular.

1. Start in the larger screen-only view. Choose **Device view** to try the Rev E model and folding right-side crank.
2. Open **Menu → Rod workshop** and choose a technique. Float drift asks you to distinguish a nibble from a bite. The spoon needs a short wind followed by a pause. The jig needs a lift followed by a drop. Each technique has on-screen cues.
3. Tap to cast and strike, then drag the round knob clockwise to retrieve. When the fish runs, ease off or pay out. When it swims toward you, take up slack quickly. A landed fish moves into the collection.
4. To try motion controls, open **Menu → Motion bench**, enable motion and grant browser permission. Hold the phone still in your playing position and capture rest. Raise its far edge 30–50°, hold still and capture lift. Resume, raise and sweep down to cast. Touch controls remain available.
5. To try a hosted shared event, open the private Site in two separate browser profiles or on two phones signed into the owner account. Choose the same fishing spot and join **Menu → Shared event** in each. The goal is eight catches per three-minute round.

The collection, rod setup and settings are saved in the browser on each device. A real handheld uses its own save. The hosted browser event and the physical prototype’s local-network event host run separate rooms. The battery badge on the phone is marked SIM and can be changed in Motion bench.
