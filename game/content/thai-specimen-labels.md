# Pocket Reel Thai names — verified names and attribution

The Thai overlay is additive and browser-only. Stable IDs, art, English original, scientific identifiers, saves, and fictional labels are unchanged. No firmware change.

## Coverage and naming hierarchy

All 2,545 catalog entries have Thai-script display in Thai mode. The 60 existing, unkeyed labels (including fictional fish) are unchanged. Among 2,485 keyed scientific entries, 229 have exact-name vernacular evidence (13 initial + 71 first audit + 23 first follow-up + 87 second follow-up + 12 wetlands follow-up + 15 register follow-up + 8 common-tier freshwater follow-up); the remaining 2,256 use a clearly marked approximate **ชื่อวิทยาศาสตร์ถอดเสียง**, not a verified Thai common name. Attested regional names can be shared by multiple taxa. A family label is kept as family information and caught-only search, never promoted to a species label. The exact Latin and stable ID remain the identity authority. Runtime does not rename an uncaught silhouette or expose an undiscovered identity in name search.

The 71 new rows combine ONEP regional taxon/checklist records, Department of Fisheries species-manual Appendix A, and a separate marine source pass. Each shipped row in [the overlay](thai-specimen-labels.json) records stable ID, GBIF key, exact binomial, name and cited source(s). Regional batches overlapped at IDs 1046 and 1290: paired same-taxon labels retained at 1046; the more specific Department of Fisheries name preferred at 1290, without promoting a generic group name. Appendix A overlaps at IDs 204 and 583 use the appendix display and keep attested same-taxon field/ONEP aliases. Provenance verifies a name, not where the game fish was caught. Source collection is still open and no simulated test establishes real device Thai font fit.

[The reading-aid generator](build-thai-scientific-transcriptions.py) deterministically applies project grapheme and syllable rules to the exact catalog binomial. It is an approximate phonetic aid, not an official standard or a guarantee of scientific pronunciation. Regenerate it from repository source with `python game/content/build-thai-scientific-transcriptions.py`. The browser build requires exact keyed-catalog coverage and NFC Thai script. Preserve English, exact binomial, stable art, save and caught-only search across future source-backed promotions.

## Follow-up: 23 verified labels

The 23-label batch is additive. It draws on Department of Fisheries publications and exact ONEP taxon records, with four independently checked [national ONEP workbook](https://chm-thai.onep.go.th/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/5_Fish_04NOV2025.xlsx) rows. ## Follow-up: 87 verified labels

It joins exact scientific names and species-level identities; no English-to-Thai guesses, fictional additions, source images, raw research files, occurrence data, or location claims are included. Source links and local record locators stay with each label. Only independently corroborated Wikidata-discovered names were promoted, not raw handles or database labels. The label may reflect regional usage; stable IDs, scientific names, families, English and player saves are unchanged.

- **29 Aqualib matches:** [Department of Fisheries ornamental-fish dataset](https://data.go.th/dataset/aquatic-animal), resource `28b042fd-0804-4020-9f99-343d97bb3b87`, exact public source version ETL 11 November 2026, CSV SHA-256 `1c42a69cfedda99e9fcda18a929c76d3959bcda955c94f1156809100ae89be1f`. Corroborators include ONEP and academic/government field records. No cultivar names, generic family names, or misleading historical glass-catfish usage were copied from Aqualib.
- **26 Wikidata-assisted matches:** 7 from the first pilot and 19 from the broader sample. Match exact P225 scientific name with the catalog's legacy GBIF P846 key and species rank. [Wikidata structured data](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) is CC0, but a Thai tag or an exact entity does not establish a local vernacular. The cited species account, diagnostic comparison or primary report is the name authority. Examples: ปลาผีเสื้อถ้ำ (#457) and ปลากระสูบสาละวิน (#1781).

The common shark label ฉลามกบ was not promoted where it would cross several species.

## Holds, not fixes
- Kryptopterus vitreolus is not K. bicirrhis: the primary [species description](https://mapress.com/zt/article/view/zootaxa.3630.2.6) documents the old misidentification. Keep ปลาก้างพระร่วง on #2354, not #805; don't propagate it from historical lists.
- Channa gachua (#363): DOF shows ก้าง, but [Thai primary survey](https://www.biodiversityjournal.com/pdf/6%282%29_513-516.pdf) flags Thai specimens as taxonomically uncertain between C. gachua and C. limbata. Hold rather than rely on exact historic spelling.
- Pristis microdon (#1998): [TH-BIF record](https://thbif.onep.go.th/taxons/detail/33742) and DOF display different Thai names. Both may be valid vernacular synonyms, but first resolve accepted species usage, then curate aliases instead of silently treating the difference as an error.
- Several independent exact rows share weak common labels (e.g. Opsarius koratensis / O. pulchellus both น้ำหมึก); don't assume Thai names identify unique species. Keep Latin detail and caught-only discovery search.
