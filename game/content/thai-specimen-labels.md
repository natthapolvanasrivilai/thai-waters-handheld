# Pocket Reel Thai names — verified names and attribution
 
The Thai overlay is additive and browser-only. Stable IDs, art, English original, scientific identifiers, saves, and fictional labels are unchanged. No firmware change.

## Coverage
Of 2,545 catalog entries: **60 existing Thai labels** (not an independent audit of all 60), **11 source-backed additions** (3 phase 1 + 8 phase 2), **345 family labels** (not species names), and **2,129 explicitly unconfirmed**. Adding 8 replaces six family labels and two missing entries. Runtime tests check the 8 expected row changes and rejection on a conflicting key or binomial. No simulated test establishes real device Thai font fit.

## DOF source and join
- Thai Department of Fisheries, [Thai Aquatic Animals Data](https://data.go.th/en/dataset/item_cb5d392d-2824-4ac4-a171-2c4796ce81c2), resource ce7f4a78-71db-4754-9084-edca971903bd, DGA metadata says Creative Commons Attributions (version not shown). Original CSV bytes not reliably reachable. [ThaiGovData article](https://thaigovdata.com/en/datasets/item-cb5d392d-2824-4ac4-a171-2c4796ce81c2) links its archived exact-source copy [DOF_fishbase.csv](https://thaigovdata.com/dl/9306): 323,717 bytes, 162 rows, UTF-8 BOM, SHA256 c3a6ba99d9724fd2608bf20d9833c1cbc7e561c6a804208feee28a5a6771284b. Checking mirrored attribution and structure is not proof of byte parity with original.
- Only exact binomial matches with a Thai name were extracted; **83 candidates**, seven corroborated and included in the optional overlay. (One additional official-PDF entry, A. percula, is not in the CSV.) The other 76 CSV candidates remain review-only. No photos, occurrence data, body text, or FishBase data copied into the game. Normalization uses NFC (not NFKC, which decomposed Thai ำ into a different glyph sequence).
- [Official Department of Fisheries clownfish publication](https://www4.fisheries.go.th/local/file_document/20161208141836_file.pdf): explicitly distinguishes A. ocellaris (ปลาการ์ตูนส้มขาว) from A. percula (ปลาการ์ตูนเพอร์คูลาร์). A. polymnus uses ปลาการ์ตูนอานม้า in the main text/introduction, corroborated by the DOF CSV; the section header has inconsistent spelling. Distinct color morphs of A. ocellaris/A. polymnus do not create new species.
- [DOF sea-bass report](https://www4.fisheries.go.th/local/file_document/20191028104026_1_file.pdf) corroborates ปลากะพงขาว for Lates calcarifer; [ONEP TH-BIF record](https://thbif.onep.go.th/taxons/taxon_detail/Epalzeorhynchos%20bicolor) corroborates ปลาทรงเครื่อง for E. bicolor.

## Holds, not fixes
- Kryptopterus vitreolus is not K. bicirrhis: the primary [species description](https://mapress.com/zt/article/view/zootaxa.3630.2.6) documents the old misidentification. Keep ปลาก้างพระร่วง on #2354, not #805; don't propagate it from historical lists.
- Channa gachua (#363): DOF shows ก้าง, but [Thai primary survey](https://www.biodiversityjournal.com/pdf/6%282%29_513-516.pdf) flags Thai specimens as taxonomically uncertain between C. gachua and C. limbata. Hold rather than rely on exact historic spelling.
- Pristis microdon (#1998): [TH-BIF record](https://thbif.onep.go.th/taxons/detail/33742) and DOF display different Thai names. Both may be valid vernacular synonyms, but first resolve accepted species usage, then curate aliases instead of silently treating the difference as an error.
- Several independent exact rows share weak common labels (e.g. Opsarius koratensis / O. pulchellus both น้ำหมึก); don't assume Thai names identify unique species. Keep Latin detail and caught-only discovery search.
- Next: acquire original [DOF ornamental fish CSV/JSON](https://data.go.th/dataset/aqualib) (publisher lists Creative Commons Attributions and scientific/Thai columns; portal/API timed out when fetching metadata/bytes). Cross-check high-traffic species and contested names against separate official pages; mark aliases by confirmed taxon, then run browser/saved-game checks in supported runtime. Some global species may simply lack an attested Thai name; retain honest fallback.
