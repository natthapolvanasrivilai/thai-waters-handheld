# Attributed Thai fish field records

The field-evidence supplement adds **377 real fish** to the previous 2,168 entries. The playable roster now has **2,545 entries**, including eight fictional species and one freshwater prawn. All shipped IDs 0–2167 and their game records were preserved. New IDs 2168–2544 are appended in scientific-name order. [Per-species evidence](thai-field-evidence.csv) · [Source collections and citations](thai-field-datasets.csv)

Of the additions, 369 have physical specimen vouchers. Eight have photographed research-grade observations on different dates by different observers. Among the 369 voucher-backed names, 282 also have an exact regional checklist match and at least two additional CC0 records in nearby Southeast Asia. The remaining 87 are supported by Thai type material (54) or by physical vouchers held by distinct institutions from different collecting dates (33). Examples include *Betta pallida*, the glass catfish *Kryptopterus vitreolus*, and *Paracanthocobitis phuketensis*. ## Scope and selection

The November 19, 2026 snapshot queried all 40 fish orders represented in the earlier Thai catalog. It selected country TH, present records, preserved specimens or human observations, and CC0 or CC BY licenses. GBIF returned 2,875 distinct species keys, of which 972 were absent from the original accepted-key table. The importer checked all 4,806 country records for these candidates. It pages through every species facet and every record page. [GBIF occurrence API](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/v1/occurrence)

Each accepted row has a species-level determination, a GBIF accepted species key, and a family and order. The record must have a compatible georeference or a named Thai locality. Specimen vouchers require a catalog number and institution. Photo observations must come from the iNaturalist research-grade dataset. A qualifying regional match uses the ASEAN fish checklist and corroborating CC0 occurrence records from Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore or Vietnam. The source records describe reported occurrences. They do not establish modern abundance, country-wide nativity or use of a particular game destination. Rarity, presentation, encounter time and spot remain game parameters. [ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity archive v7.13](https://ipt.aseanbiodiversity.org/archive.do?r=aseanfishspecieslist&v=7.13) · [iNaturalist research-grade data](https://doi.org/10.15468/ab3s5x)

The importer screens fuzzy identifications, country and coordinate conflicts, captive, trading and aquaculture contexts, as well as the Thailand Global Register of Introduced and Invasive Species and documented aquaculture introductions. A Mexican Pacific collection with erroneous Thailand codes, the European conger, the brown moray and the Red Sea grouper were excluded during review. The alternate tinfoil-barb spelling maps to the shipped game fish. ## Attribution and reproduction

For each additional species the evidence CSV names the publisher dataset, numeric GBIF occurrence IDs, applicable licenses and credit. Pipe-separated values correspond across the columns for taxa with two vouchers. The dataset CSV gives the publisher's collection title, DOI and citation. The project selected records, reconciled identities and added the authored gameplay settings. Country observations and attributed catalog metadata come from the 29 linked data collections. Source photographs and label scans remain at their publishers. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) · [Record ledger](thai-field-evidence.csv) · [Collection credits](thai-field-datasets.csv)

Supply the pinned ASEAN and GRIIS archives and a writable cache directory. A fresh cache queries current GBIF data, which can change as source collections are updated. An unchanged cache reproduces this snapshot. The importer verifies the checklist archive hashes before filtering. [Importer](import_field_evidence.py) · [GRIIS archive v1.4](https://cloud.gbif.org/griis/archive.do?r=griis-thailand&v=1.4)
