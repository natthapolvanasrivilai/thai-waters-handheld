# Thai fishing atlas

Release 0.3 contains 117 selectable locations with stable numeric IDs. The initial four preserve the legacy progression families. Mae Ngat Reservoir replaces the imagined Chiang Mai Pond. The 96 inland entries name real rivers, creeks, lakes, wetlands and reservoirs in the Thai Department of Fisheries guide, table 5. The 21 saltwater entries use named provincial fishing waters within the department's Gulf and Andaman reporting areas. They identify stretches of coast rather than individual fishing piers. Each record has an independently written fictional scene and its supporting geographic source.

Every location has a fixed composition selected by stable destination ID. Two bytes per location select a water form and a story motif. A deterministic renderer builds the local skyline, banks, reflection, framing vegetation, and landmark. Dawn, day, dusk, and night relight that view. These scenes are fictional illustrations of the named waters. The handheld expands the current 240 × 160 indexed scene into the existing 480 × 320 LCD framebuffer and keeps one RGB565 background for animation. Scenery scratch uses the framebuffer itself. The previous five index maps and their color tables occupied 778,240 bytes before linking. The verification script checks all 468 rendered views and compares coarse structural compositions within each time period. Scores reuse regional themes with deterministic local arrangements. Encounter pools are game designs. Blue-clawed river prawns appear in the lowland river pool at dusk/night. Freshwater stingrays appear in the big-river pool at dawn/night. Saltwater adds ten marine species including queenfish, cobia, giant trevally and sailfish. The journal records counts and best length for 2,168 entries including 2,159 fish, one freshwater prawn and eight fictional creatures. The [country checklist](content/national-checklist.md) documents the source records. The catch card uses a species-specific measurement and estimated weight. See [catch measurements and sources](catch-measurements.md). Story lines are fiction and the atlas is a game, not an access or angling-permit directory.
