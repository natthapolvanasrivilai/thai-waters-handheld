# Rev E manufacturing and market-access review
Engineering planning record • 18 November 2026 • Thailand, United States and European Union • prototype disposition: physical tests and market approvals outstanding.

## Decisions before a tooling order

1. **Resolve the radio architecture first.** Waveshare 31137 uses an ESP32-S3R8 and an onboard antenna. A reusable modular radio approval for this exact board, antenna and handheld exposure configuration has not been established. Ask for the grant, integration instructions, antenna details, schematics, regional reports and written change-notification policy. Obtain two lab quotations: testing this complete radio design, and integrating a documented radio module on a carrier. Freeze the antenna keep-out, hand position, metallic inserts, magnet, motor and harness routing only after a closed-case RF pre-scan. [Manufacturer board specification](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B)
2. **Design the pack and reel for physical testing.** The Adafruit 258 two-wire pack is a prototype reference. Its current CAD allowance is 0.40 mm to the bare cell envelope. Obtain maximum pack, protection-board, lead and aging dimensions before fixing the pocket. Specify a keyed connector, strain relief, a compression-free replacement path and a documented temperature-based charging strategy validated for the chosen pack. Validate the AXP2101 4.20 V / 300 mA configuration through startup, USB hot plug, charging while playing and worst-case radio/audio/rumble duty. Use a captive magnet and a retained pivot fastener. Measure retention after drops and reel cycling. [Pack specification](https://www.adafruit.com/product/258) · [EU battery replaceability guidance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2025/214/oj/eng)
3. **Set the intended age and market before packaging.** Document play appeal, marketing, the pocketable form, accessible magnet, screw and hinge pinch gap. A label alone cannot settle age classification. Reserve a durable identification panel, batch or serial identifier and space for the appropriate market artwork. Keep mains adapters and RTC backup cells as explicit SKU decisions. [Thai toy scope](https://a.tisi.go.th/t/?n=5220) · [US toy guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Toy-Safety)
4. **Keep programming access and regulatory configuration distinct.** Provide documented sensor APIs and USB recovery. Keep the commercial radio profile, country parameters, signed release process and tested maximum power identifiable by version. Reassess on changes to antenna, radio modes, transmit power, enclosure or battery. Design a factory test mode for continuous representative traffic, display switching, audio and rumble. Budget for support and vulnerability reporting while the product is sold. [EU CRA implementation](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/cyber-resilience-act-implementation)

## Closing the design review
At EVT, preserve photographs and measured clearances on the exact board and pack. Capture current, temperature, USB behavior, near-field emissions and antenna performance in the folded and deployed configurations, using a hand grip and the intended cable. Log reel magnet status over a complete revolution with the motor at each intensity. Calculate runtime from measured play, Wi-Fi event, update and clock-holdover currents. Establish acceptance limits with the selected component specifications and lab.

At DVT, run final radio, EMC, exposure, ESD and applicable safety tests on the production-intent materials, software, packaging and accessories. Give labs the hardware revision, country profiles, representative accessories and photographs. At PVT, lock drawings, approved suppliers, factory firmware hash, labels, programming procedure and lot traceability. Request separate quotes for pre-scan, formal tests, possible re-test and filing. Track market changes against the intended launch date.

The present evidence supports a fit-print build. Quantity-production approval, certification numbers, official marks, measured runtime with Wi-Fi, and a shipment authorization remain open.
