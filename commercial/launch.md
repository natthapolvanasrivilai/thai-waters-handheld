# Thai Waters launch and pricing hypothesis

**Lead with the rumble-and-rod version for adult Thai anglers and handheld-game collectors.** The sales demonstration should let a visitor wind a loose line, feel a prawn picking, and then lift a bottom-holding ray. Finish by opening the fish journal over the water. A short session demonstrates the value of the right-side reel and dedicated hardware even when the visitor already owns a phone.

## Suggested direct retail prices

| Configuration | US, before sales tax | Thailand, consumer price including modeled VAT |
|---|---:|---:|
| Core handheld with captive receiver | $139 | ฿4,490 |
| Core with rumble | $159 | ฿5,090 |
| Core with removable rod | $149 | ฿4,790 |
| Full rod and rumble set | **$169** | **฿5,490** |

These are price tests. Start by featuring the full set at a physical demonstration and use the base model to test price sensitivity. Take interest registrations with explicit price and expected availability. Wait for validated hardware, fulfillment, market access and a lead-time commitment before accepting paid orders.

At 100 full sets the Thai factory cost is **$98.93 per good unit**. The US landed assumption is **$103.43**. The proposed prices leave **฿949.15 in Thailand** and **$35.44 in the US** per DTC sale after the modeled gateway, warranty, support, handling and acquisition costs. At a single-off build, Thai contribution is approximately **−฿60**, before recovering program spend. At 1,000 full sets, modeled contribution rises to **฿1,209.47 / $43.38**. The combined $34,000 engineering, compliance and tooling reserve would require **984 Thai sales or 784 US sales** at those 1,000-unit economics. The FX scenario is ฿35 per USD. At 100, factory economics and the Thai price imply a Thai-only program break-even of 1,254 sales at the same unit economics. All four configurations and volumes are editable in the workbook. ## First 100-unit route

| Stage | Execution | Proposed decision threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Bench-to-demo | Give 12 independently recruited adult players a supervised playable unit. Include Thai/English speakers, experienced anglers and first-time players. Counterbalance starting fish and rod configuration. | At least 9/12 land a catch unassisted within five minutes. Record unclear gestures, missed strikes, fatigue, accidental power presses and grip movement. Resolve failures before reservations. |
| Thai price test | Run the same 20-second vertical clip on two Thai landing pages offering the FULL set at ฿4,990 and ฿5,490. Show the price, real hardware and expected ship window. Randomize visitors by session. | Suggested minimum 150 qualified visits per arm. Compare verified reservation rate, expected contribution per visitor and opt-out rate. A 500-baht reduction also reduces contribution; higher clicks alone do not justify it. |
| Retail-counter trial | Lend serial-tracked demonstration units to two independent tackle shops and one indie-game venue after explicit agreements. Use a separate QR per location with a direct manufacturer checkout. | Count completed demos, repeat plays, opt-in reservations, actual paid conversion after release and repairs by venue. Negotiate a referral fee against contribution, including its tax and return treatment. |
| Pilot shipment | Release a limited DTC drop against measured assembly yield. Ship the Thai-language onboarding card first and invite players to one scheduled river event. | Track actual cost, acquisition, returns, hardware failure type, 7/30-day return-to-play rates, support minutes, firmware update success and shipping damage. Investigate if Thai CAC exceeds ฿220 or US CAC exceeds $12 in the baseline assumptions. |

At 50% of US MSRP, full-set wholesale contribution is **−$25.64 at 100** and **−$17.70 at 1,000**. A $169 full-set street price therefore favors DTC or a negotiated small referral fee in this model. At 1,000 units, break-even wholesale MSRP is about **$206** before recovering program NRE. Require either a higher retailer invoice, a redesign that removes at least $17.70 of US landed cost per unit at the same shelf price, or revised terms before signing standard wholesale orders.

Use photos of the shipping prototype, close audio of its reel and footage from a verified real Thai location. Obtain rights and permissions for any third-party location images, names, music or organization endorsements. The web demo can demonstrate controls and music. For iPhone viewers, demonstrate actual fish rumble on filmed hardware. Public marketing should use measured battery runtime and actual multiplayer scale after validation.

The Thai workbook treats the listed consumer price as VAT-inclusive at a 7% planning input. Reconfirm seller registration, the rate in force, customs and display conventions before an invoice or advertised price. Regional cloud operation, account service, customer shipping and a reseller's fees require written terms. [Royal Thai Government VAT policy context](https://www.thaigov.go.th/en/news/166969)
