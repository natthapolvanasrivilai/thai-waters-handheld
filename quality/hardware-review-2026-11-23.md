# Hardware review · 23 November 2026

**Engineering review. Powered assembly, production, and shipment remain on hold.** The two identifiers below refer to separate artifacts. The R0012 connector study uses the 147-member dressed STEP from the R0011 enclosure lineage. R19b is the X1C print that broke during support removal. It includes a revised housing, encoder carrier and single RGB diffuser. Transfer of the connector and daughterboard placements into that housing needs a matched enclosure model and a fresh clearance check.

## Inspect the connector

Use the Play motion control or move the slider from seated through latch release to withdrawal. The four W1 trunks and their tap locations use the same installed coordinates throughout. The shorter W4 wires attached to the crimp contacts bend as the plug moves. The rear terminal-position lock stays installed. The live clearance is the distance to the nearest surface at the indicated test station. The numerical bound covers the intervening positions for the rigid plug against W1. Flexible leads and other neighbors have separately reported sampled results.

## What limits withdrawal

The rigid plug has **3.240 mm** minimum at the sampled stations and a **3.156 mm** lower bound across the full 8.25 mm stroke. With the display lifted, the closest flexible lead is W4 GND against fixed W1 SCL at the seated end. The native-solid check gives **0.347 mm**. With the display seated, W4 SCL contacts A5 by **0.500 mm** of withdrawal. The display and J8 must be released and the J8 plug supported in its original pose for this service study. The SDA lead reaches a 0.916 mm centerline bend radius. The finished harness still needs a bend and cycle test. [Installed section, tolerances and physical holds](../hardware/mechanical/TW-A-0001-R0012/README.md)

I also registered the owner’s R19b shell to the four H1 screw datums and checked it against 17 seated R0012 components or component groups. All 17 cleared the housing material. W4 GND came within 1.943 mm of that shell during the sampled stroke. The PCB, wire-to-wire and latch limitations above still apply. [Cross-revision housing evidence](../hardware/verification/r0012/r19b-shell-fit.json)

## Print investigation

The owner identified **R19b** as the failed print. The recovered four-plate `Pocket-Reel-R19b-X1C-Fit-Test.3mf` has SHA-256 `dcfc3c9c0725d91c54026f1e65f5b9855a036b35f61483d5b1dfcfb471db546c`. Keep the archived R19b kit withdrawn. The acceptance record needs damage-free brim and support removal and a spring-free inert assembly. The screw-insertion fracture and the H1-3 access interference have separate records. [Print history and withdrawal criteria](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/issues/19) · [Original photographs](first-article/2026-11-20-first-fit-print/README.md) · [Fastener fracture](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/issues/23) · [Driver access](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/issues/27)

**First print to try.** The R20 first-article project is a single eight-object X1C plate. It uses a spring-free thumbwheel to exercise the shaft, a reinforced keyed carrier, a cropped section of the actual floor pocket and small retaining parts. Native toolpaths show **no support or brim** on these eight items. The first layer of the wheel grows from 14.3 to 357.2 mm² of model contact. The complete housing and folding crank remain separate engineering work. [Download the R20 first-article 3MF](https://github.com/natthapolvanasrivilai/thai-waters-handheld/releases/download/r0012-service-fit-review-1/Pocket-Reel-R20-First-Article-X1C.3mf) · [Native projects, removal evidence and inspection criteria](../hardware/mechanical/pocket-reel-r19b-removal-trial/README.md) · [Archived R19b source for reproduction](../hardware/mechanical/history/r19b-withdrawn/WITHDRAWN-R19b-source.3mf)
