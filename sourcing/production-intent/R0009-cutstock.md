# R0009 prototype cutstock and packout specifications

These reference specifications cover one dressed prototype including the detachable handle and rumble option. Paper items and unqualified cutstock carry engineering allocations. A converter still needs sample approval and final artwork or tooling before ordering a production lot.

## C4

Reserve one low-profile battery retention pad set. Trial on an inert gauge and verify removability, pouch clearance and compatibility with the delivered pack before installing. The speaker acoustic gasket is charged solely to SP1-G. No additional speaker retention pad has a released envelope.

## C5

Cut serviceable electrical insulating tape from shop stock for the battery wire bridge slots, power-key soldered leads and exposed USB carrier lands. Size on the actual build, keep spring travel and connector access clear, and record material and cut lengths. C5 covers tape only. Adhesive staking is USB-BOND and the four haptic branch joint seals are C8.

## C6

Reserve one electrical insulating trim shim for the power cap if measurement after assembly shows it is needed. Select and measure thickness on the first article. Keep the cap travel free and verify release with the case closed.

## C8

Overcoat each of the four stripped, soldered W1-to-W4 tee taps individually. The CAD defines a 2.8 mm window and covers the lateral conductor as well as the exposed trunk. See [tap geometry](../../hardware/electronics/harness-r0003/place_w1.py). Qualify an electrical insulating overcoat compatible with the conductor jacket and record cure, adhesion, coverage and separation after flexing. Budget per completed joint. Reserve four joints on the rumble build. Straight adapter splices use C1.

## PK1

One E-flute tuck carton plus one neutral shipping sleeve. Size the carton around the qualified PK2 cradle, handheld, detached handle, cable and sleeved key. Converter dieline and board specification await sample fit and drop testing.

## PK2

One folded paperboard cradle. Retain the handheld and separate the cable, tool and detached grip from the screen. Converter dieline and insert retention remain open.

## PK3

One two-sided Thai/English quick-start and support card. Print the approved revision and verified support QR for the destination. Artwork, cut size and printer quote remain open.

## PK4

One unit identification label set. Verify model, serial and lot against the assembled unit and record the applied artwork revision. Label artwork, stock and adhesive require approval.

## PK5-S

One folded paper tool sleeve to contain the PK5 hex key, separate from the display. Confirm tool retention and fit inside PK2. Dieline and converter quote remain open.
