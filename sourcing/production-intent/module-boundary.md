# Purchased subassemblies

The manufacturing plan buys the assembled A1, A2 and optional A3 boards. Their internal chips, passives, connectors and SMT assembly are included in their respective purchase prices. This boundary keeps component quantities and recurring costs from being counted twice.

| Purchased reference | Included functional hardware | Evidence / negotiation |
|---|---|---|
| A1, one Waveshare 31137 | ESP32-S3R8 processor/radio and stacked PSRAM, 16 MB W25Q128JVSIQ flash, integrated IPS/capacitive panel, QMI8658 IMU, PCF85063 RTC, AXP2101 power/charge controller, ES8311 codec, audio output stage, microphone, USB-C, board headers, BOOT/RESET/PWR switches, antenna and associated passives. The board also has expansion interfaces. | Match the A1 silk to the supplier schematic. Ask for exact board revision, panel sources, RF construction, subassembly AVL, test report and PCN agreement. Inventory each connected peripheral on incoming samples. [Waveshare resources](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B) |
| SP1, one per A1 | Supplied speaker and two-pin plug-in lead. | Record supplied impedance, dimensions and connection. Short-shipped kits require a quoted substitute and acoustic qualification. [Selected retailer](https://www.robotshop.com/products/waveshare-esp32-s3-35-ips-touch-display-development-board-type-b-320480-camera-interface-wi-fi-bluetooth-5-no-case) |
| A2, one Adafruit 6357 | AS5600 Hall angle IC and assembled breakout including support circuitry and headers/connectors as supplied. | Check exact breakout revision and cable routing. Check output connection and branch address on the assembled unit. [Adafruit board schematics](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-drv2605-haptic-controller-breakout/downloads) |
| B1, one Adafruit 258 | Protected 1S pack including factory protection electronics, attached leads and JST-PH connector. | Preserve the supplied pack. Qualify its two-wire thermal/charge behavior in the closed enclosure. Obtain exact lot, protection and shipping evidence. [Battery manufacturer listing](https://www.adafruit.com/product/258) |

For an in-house replacement controller, obtain the schematic, production layout, controlled resistor/capacitor tolerances, approved equivalents, footprint libraries, layer stack, panelization, SPI/AOI/test fixtures and vendor rights for the proposed ECAD. Build and cost that separate assembly at component level before committing to module replacement. The current build pack includes fabrication data for the small top-key carrier only.
